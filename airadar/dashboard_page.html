Key findings

Fastest-growing apps

Score is the evidence-weighted percentile of each app's growth signals (0 to 1), pulled toward 0.5 when evidence is thin. Evidence type says how the strongest signal was obtained. Flagged rows gained over half their total in a week; their weight is halved until a person or a re-check confirms them.

Directions

Top-quartile share is the fraction of a direction's scored apps that rank in the top quarter of all apps. It resists one category having many slow members. Status compares each direction's mean score with the previous run: accelerating or cooling means a move of at least 0.05.

Share of apps in the top quartile

Bar length is top-quartile share. The label gives the number of apps and, once there is a previous run, whether the direction is accelerating or cooling.

Innovation hypotheses

Rule-based patterns in the direction table, graded as assumptions. They point research at a gap; they do not establish one.

How the radar is doing

Self-tuning

—: —.

Each day's ranking is saved as a forecast and scored after 7 days against the growth that followed. The tuner moves one setting one step only if a backtest beats the current settings by a fixed margin on most past days. Its yardstick uses fixed default settings, so it cannot move its own target.
