# Reported growth: qualitative trends (collected 2026-10-19)

Gathered from web search result summaries only. WebFetch could not resolve any host in this session, so no page was opened directly; check figures before publishing.

1. Coding agents are still the fastest-growing paid category, and spending is moving to autonomous and cloud agents. Cursor went from $3B (late Apr) to $4B (early Jun), and about 75% of that is enterprise. Cognition went from $492M (May) to ~$900M (Sep). Lovable reached $600M, and Emergent and Base44 are each above $100M.
   https://dealroom.co/news/134107-cursor-tops-4b-annualized-revenue/
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/08/cognition-hits-48b-valuation-signaling-investors-believe-ai-coding-is-far-from-a-winner-take-all-market/
2. Coding is consolidating. SpaceX/xAI has a $60B acquisition of Cursor, reported as an option by some sources and as completed by others. Cognition rebranded Windsurf as Devin Desktop and bought Poke. Wix bought Base44. Investors still treat the market as having room for more than one winner.
   https://dealroom.co/charts/cursor-acquisition-60b?preview=1
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/why-cognition-bought-poke-ai-personality-is-becoming-a-competitive-advantage/
3. The assistant market is splitting three ways. Claude is the breakout. Claude's share of gen-AI web traffic went from 2.3% (Oct 2025) to 9.6% (Sep 2026), and claude.ai visits were up about 540% year on year. ChatGPT's traffic share fell from 71.8% to 56.4%, but its visits rose again in September (5.9B). Gemini passed 1B MAU, but its web visits have declined since June.
   https://fourweekmba.com/ai-claudes-ai-web-traffic-share-quadruples-in-a-year-to-9-6/
   https://www.forbes.com/sites/conormurray/2026/10/03/chatgpts-traffic-roars-back-after-year-of-stagnation/
4. Revenue figures are being questioned. The FT reported OpenAI near $50B annualized at end-September, not the ~$70B that had circulated. The gap is about whether sales through cloud partners are counted, which Anthropic does. Treat company "run-rate" and "ARR" claims across vendors as not directly comparable.
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/openais-revenue-is-reportedly-20-billion-less-than-previously-projected/
5. The new a16z Top 100 (7th edition, Oct 5) has only 11 new entrants, the fewest of any edition. It adds US card-spend data: paid subscriptions to ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude reached 4.5% of consumers (2.1% a year earlier), and the top 1% of payers drive 19.5% of spend. Behind OpenAI and Anthropic, the spend leaders include Canva, Superhuman and Higgsfield.
   https://a16z.com/100-gen-ai-apps-7/
   https://www.odaily.news/en/newsflash/523978
6. Generative video is the consumer breakout. Higgsfield reached a $1B run-rate in about 18 months (20x in 12 months), and Runway and HeyGen each passed $200M by doubling in 5 to 8 months. Kling's Q2 revenue was up more than 200% year on year. A lot of the growth is enterprise or marketing use, not hobbyists.
   https://www.arr.club/signal/higgsfield-arr-hit-1b-with-20x-growth-in-the-past-12-months
   https://cryptobriefing.com/runway-200m-arr-ai-video-enterprise/
7. Vertical AI is compounding. In legal, Harvey reached $400M (Sep) and Legora went from $100M to $200M in six months. In healthcare, OpenEvidence reached ~$300M ARR, ad-supported, with 860k clinicians. In finance, Rogo went from $15M to $50M+ in 2026.
   https://dealroom.co/news/155480-legora-doubles-to-200m-arr-in-six-months/
   https://www.arr.club/signal/rogo-arr-hit-50m-on-track-for-100m-arr-by-next-year
8. Standalone AI browsers are retreating. OpenAI is shutting down Atlas and moving agentic browsing into ChatGPT desktop and a Chrome extension. The Browser Company/Dia went to Atlassian. Comet is pivoting to browser agents. No reliable user numbers exist for any of them.
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/openai-is-shutting-down-atlas-but-its-ai-browser-ambitions-are-still-growing/
9. The companion category is shaking out. Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 on Oct 12 with ~12M monthly users, $5.3M revenue a month and $8-9M monthly operating losses. A Disney affiliate is the stalking-horse bidder.
   https://ppc.land/character-ai-loses-8m-a-month-despite-2-5m-in-monthly-ad-sales/
10. Customer support and voice agents have reached scale. ElevenLabs is pacing at $600M ARR (valued at $22B). Sierra passed $200M, doubling in two quarters, and Decagon reached $100M. Pricing is moving to per-resolution or usage-based, and Perplexity is moving to usage-based premium tiers.
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/twenty-minutes-with-the-ceo-of-elevenlabs-now-reportedly-valued-at-22-billion/
