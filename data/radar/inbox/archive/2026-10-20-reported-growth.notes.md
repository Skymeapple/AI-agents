# Reported growth: qualitative trends (collected 2026-10-20)

All of this comes from search-result summaries. In this session WebFetch could not resolve hosts, the Tavily quota was exhausted, and direct curl was blocked, so no page was opened. Check figures against the primary pages before publishing.

1. The definition of lab revenue is now the story. Axios (Sep 29) put OpenAI near $70B ARR, up more than 70% since July 1. The FT later reported ~$50B, the difference being whether partner sales are counted net or gross. Anthropic told investors on Oct 14 that Q3 revenue was $16.8B, up ~45% QoQ, which is down from ~147% QoQ growth in Q2. It also reported an adjusted 11% operating margin and is aiming for a November IPO.

   https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-q3-revenue-16-8bn-ipo
2. OpenAI DevDay (Sep 29): ChatGPT reached 1.2B WAU, and developers can now build and share tools inside ChatGPT. OpenAI now reports Codex usage combined with ChatGPT Work (35M+ weekly), which makes Codex-only growth impossible to track. That combined figure was not recorded.
   https://fourweekmba.com/ai-chatgpt-12b-weekly-users-devday-2026-distribution/
   https://presenc.ai/research/openai-codex-usage-statistics-2026
3. Coding agents and infrastructure keep reaching $1B. Cognition's own blog says it crossed $1B run rate on Sep 25, about 2x since May. Modal raised $750M at $15.75B (Oct 14) after passing a $1B run rate, with $300M of that from agent Sandboxes. That points to agent-runtime spend as the next layer.
   https://cognition.com/blog/1b-run-rate
   https://fourweekmba.com/ai-modal-tops-1b-run-rate-raises-750m-at-15-75b-valuation/
4. Chinese model makers are monetizing through APIs, not consumer apps. Moonshot's ARR went from ~$300M in June to over $1B in August after Kimi K3, with more than 70% from API and consumer traffic shrinking. Zhipu's MaaS ARR is $1.6B and its H1 revenue was up ~400%, mostly from API. MiniMax's H1 revenue was up 283%, with the open platform up 703% against AI-native apps up 101%.
   https://aiweekly.co/alerts/moonshot-ai-tells-investors-arr-topped-1b-in-august-targets-2b-by-end-of-2026
   https://equalocean.com/news/2026090222159-zhipu-ais-h1-revenue-jumps-nearly-400-as-api-business-takes-86-5-sales

5. Personal-agent assistants are drawing mega-rounds before they have revenue. Instinct raised $1B at $10B (Sep 28) while still invite-only, with no revenue or user numbers disclosed. Town reached a unicorn valuation (Oct 19) with "millions" in ARR. Manus raised more than $500M (Oct 8) after Meta's acquisition was unwound. Use.ai, a multi-model bundle, passed 1M MAU.
   https://runtimewire.com/article/instinct-noah-shinn-1-billion-series-c-personal-ai-agent
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/19/ai-assistant-startup-town-nabs-unicorn-valuation-four-months-after-launch
   https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/chinas-manus-raises-over-500m-in-first-funding-round-since-split-with-meta/
6. AI unit costs are hitting app-layer margins. Canva cut its 2026 growth forecast from 30% to 20% after relying too heavily on frontier models. It says it has since cut per-task AI cost by ~90%, and backers reportedly marked its valuation down by about $10B.
   https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/canva-startup-growth-ai-costs-revenue-forecast-by-third/
7. Web traffic shares are settling. In September Similarweb had ChatGPT at 56.4% (5.9B visits), Gemini at 25.0% (2.6B) and Claude at 9.6% (~1.0B, #31 site overall). ChatGPT has been regaining share since April. DeepSeek (~354M in Aug) and Grok (~286M in Sep, per FirstPage Sage) are flat.
   https://www.visualcapitalist.com/ranked-worlds-most-popular-ai-tools-2026/
   https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/deepseek-statistics/
8. Evaluation and infrastructure rounds: Arena raised $200M at $3.1B (Oct 8) on consumption revenue above $100M. Stripe agreed to buy OpenRouter for more than $7B in August; Sacra estimates OpenRouter at ~$140M run-rate (not recorded, since it is an estimate), with 25T tokens a week.

9. Vertical agents: EliseAI (property management and healthcare) passed $200M ARR in June and raised $350M at $4B on Sep 29. RapidCanvas says its ARR is up 8x in six quarters. Harvey's $550M round closed Oct 1 with ARR still at $400M, which is not new.
