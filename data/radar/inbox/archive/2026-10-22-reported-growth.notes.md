# Reported growth: qualitative trends (collected 2026-10-22)

Everything here comes from WebSearch result summaries. No page was opened: Tavily returned 432 (over quota), and WebFetch failed with ENOTFOUND on cognition.com. That means no source_url from the 2026-10-20 file could be checked at source. All 12 observations are snippet-only, and the yield is below target because most queries returned figures from before the 90-day window or third-party estimates (mostly Sacra), which I left out.

- Vertical enterprise AI keeps doubling at scale. Legora said at its $550M Series E (Oct 21, $8.8B) that ARR is over $200M, up from $100M in April. Pigment (AI planning) passed $100M ARR, doubling year over year, alongside a $250M Series E (Oct 20–21).

- Voice AI is producing many mid-size ARR milestones outside the big labs. Omilia reported more than $60M live ARR (Aug 6). Fish Audio reported $21M ARR and 8M users on a $52M seed (Jul 28). fonio.ai reported $10M ARR, growing more than 30% a month (Aug 11).

- Coding agents are now customers of infrastructure. The Information (via Dealroom) reports Vercel at about $600M annualized, up from $500M in July and up 148% YoY. Agents account for about half of new business and about half of 6M daily deployments. The $600M figure is not recorded because its date is unclear.

- Consumer agent valuations are running far ahead of disclosed usage. Instinct raised $1B at $10B (Sep 28), about a month after a $2.5B Series B. It is still invite-only with "more than 100,000 users" per Dealroom, and it disclosed no revenue. Town reached $1B (Oct 19) disclosing only "7-digit" ARR and 4x paying customers since July (not recorded).

It expects at least $70B by year end and cites 77% Q3 run-rate growth. Anthropic did not update its $65B July run rate with its Q3 figure ($16.8B revenue, +45% QoQ). The ~$74B figure is a SemiAnalysis estimate (not recorded).
