# Reported growth: qualitative trends (collected 2026-10-23)

Every figure here comes from WebSearch result summaries. No page was opened: WebFetch failed with ENOTFOUND on techcrunch.com, and Tavily returned 432 (over quota). There are 11 observations, all snippet-only, which is below the 15-observation target. Most queries surfaced figures from before 2026-07-25, Sacra or other third-party estimates, or numbers already in the 10-19 to 10-22 files (Legora, Modal, Chai, Meshy, Cognition, Genspark, Otter), and those were left out.

- **Incumbents are now reporting AI-only ARR as its own line.**
  - Podium reports AI-agent ARR above $150M (Oct 22), reached in 2.5 years against 8 years for its core software business.
  - Salesforce Agentforce ARR passed $1.5B (Q2 FY27, +240% YoY), but the definition was widened that quarter.
  - Adobe reports AI-first ARR up more than 150% YoY, without disclosing the base.

  https://investor.salesforce.com/news/news-details/2026/Salesforce-Delivers-Record-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027-Results/default.aspx
  https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000796343/000079634326000147/adbeex991q326.htm

- **Consumer AI agents get big distribution fast but disclose little.**
  - Meta's Muse passed 10M accounts in the US and Canada in about six weeks (Q3 call, Oct 21), with about 25% daily use.
  - The figure is accounts set up, not MAU, and Meta gave no revenue.

  Source: https://www.wdbo.com/news/business/meta-q3-revenue-tops/LJSNKSJRPQ4XHC7YWQH2BFT3LE/

- **The second tier of creative AI is closing in on the leaders.**
  - OpenArt says it is "close to" $200M ARR in under two years and targets more than $250M by year end (Oct 21).
  - That would put it near HeyGen's and Runway's reported ~$200M.

  Source: https://www.morningstar.com/news/business-wire/20261021251869/openart-ai-hires-former-salesforce-global-head-of-brand-and-creative-scott-larson-as-company-nears-200m-arr

- **Selling evaluation and preference data to labs is a fast revenue line.**
  - Design Arena's maker reported $60M ARR, up from $5M six months earlier, on only a $7.9M seed.
  - This adds to Arena (LMArena) at $100M run-rate, recorded earlier.
  - Skild AI (robot foundation model) reports a $100M run rate 10 months after its first deployment.

  Sources:
  https://aiweekly.co/alerts/design-arena-maker-intelligence-raises-79m-seed-at-60m-arr
  https://aiweekly.co/alerts/skild-ai-hits-100m-revenue-run-rate-10-months-after-launch

- **Voice and developer-platform usage is compounding.**
  - Wispr Flow says revenue grew more than 150% quarter over quarter for four straight quarters.
  - Z.ai's MaaS registered users reached 7.4M (+144% YTD), and the company raised GLM Coding Plan prices 30% on demand.

  Sources:
  https://pulse2.com/wispr-raises-280-million-at-2-billion-valuation-as-revenue-grows-150-quarterly-and-voice-ai-push-accelerates
  https://techflowpost.com/en-US/newsletter/134252
