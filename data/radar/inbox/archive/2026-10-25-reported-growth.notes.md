## Trends

- **Agent infrastructure is the fastest-compounding layer.**
  - Daytona's sandbox revenue run rate grew 25x from January to September: https://fortune.com/2026/10/20/daytona-startup-building-the-agentic-cloud-valued-at-1-billion-in-latest-funding-round/
  - Exa, a search engine for agents, passed $100M annualized revenue, growing about 10x a year for three years: https://exa.ai/blog/100m-run-rate

- **Verification and CI for AI-written code is a new breakout category.**
  - Blacksmith's ARR grew 17x since January.
  - Its CI jobs went from 5M to more than 90M a week.
  - Source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/blacksmith-doubles-to-1b-on-sequoia-led-66m-series-c

- **AI and agent security vendors report growth multiples but rarely a dollar base.**
  - HiddenLayer: more than 10x over a year. https://siliconangle.com/?p=842675
  - Oligo: +300% YoY. https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/b1oobnxumg
  - Horizon3: $100M with more than 120% growth. https://horizon3.ai/news/press-release/horizon3-surpasses-100-million-arr/

- **Vertical agents are scaling quickly on small teams.**
  - Sela (mortgage voice agents): $10M run rate in 18 months. https://forkast.news/sela-raises-21m-for-voice-ai-agents-that-originate-1b-in-mortgage-loans-monthly/
  - Chamelio (legal): ARR up 4x in five months. https://techstartups.com/2026/09/23/chamelio-raises-26m-series-a-for-ai-legal-agents-as-arr-quadruples-in-five-months/
  - Rillet (accounting): ARR doubled in three months. https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/19/rillet-raises-100m-series-c-at-1b-valuation-2-years-after-emerging-from-stealth/
  - Owner (restaurants): more than $100M ARR. https://am.gs.com/en-au/advisors/news/press-release/2026/owner-raises-240m-gs-alts

- **Frontier-lab run-rate figures are contested.**
  - Anthropic was reported pacing above $100B, but this is a media report, not a company statement: https://www.axios.com/2026/09/18/anthropic-100-billion-revenue
  - Axios has warned that reporting on AI startup financials can be suspect: https://www.axios.com/2026/10/13/openai-anthropic-revenue-finances
  - Many startups now disclose only percentage growth with no dollar base, for example Avarra (+400% YoY): https://runtimewire.com/article/avarra-17m-series-a-ai-sales-avatars
