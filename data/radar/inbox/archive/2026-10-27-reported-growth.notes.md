## Trends
- Anthropic's S-1 (filed Oct 26) gives the first audited revenue for a frontier lab. H1 2026 revenue was $16.3B, up 1,300% YoY. About 80% comes from enterprise consumption fees. Net revenue retention is about 900%, and about 1,900 customers spend $1M+ a year. The run-rate figure for Oct 31 is left blank, and the top 5 customers are about 25% of revenue. https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/26/tech/anthropic-files-ipo ; https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/anthropics-ipo-filing-reveals-its-strategy-for-keeping-enterprise-customers-hooked/ ; https://fortune.com/2026/10/26/anthropics-s-1/
- Run-rate definitions are now the story. OpenAI told investors it had about $50B net at end-September, against about $68-70B when counted gross of partner sales. It reported +77% Q3 run-rate growth and +107% for enterprise. TechCrunch also found startups reporting contracted ARR (signed deals not yet live) as plain ARR. GMI Cloud reports contracted ARR above $600M (9x since end-2025), while its live ARR is up only about 4.5x. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/08/open-ai-revenue-nvidia-oracle-coreweave.html ; https://www.axios.com/2026/10/08/openai-50-billion-arr-anthropic-revenue-accounting ; https://startupfortune.com/the-revenue-number-startups-brag-about-might-not-be-revenue-at-all/ ; https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/gmi-cloud-raises-over-660-million-to-accelerate-global-ai-infrastructure-expansion-302894628.html
- Chinese model labs keep raising ARR guidance. Z.ai reported about $1.8B ARR in mid-September and raised its year-end target 25% to $3B after a $5B raise. Moonshot's ARR went past $1B in August. https://www.scmp.com/tech/tech-trends/article/3367755/chinas-zai-raises-revenue-target-25-after-us5-billion-cash-injection ; https://techwireasia.com/2026/09/moonshot-ai-kimi-k3-revenue/
- AI data and human-expert vendors report gross run-rates in the hundreds of millions. micro1 says its gross run rate went from $100M to $500M in eight months; its net revenue is much lower because it pays contractors. https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/09/02/ais-data-appetite-is-growing-micro1-just-hit-500m-run-rate/
- Mid-size rounds now come with growth multiples but no ARR levels. Examples: Go.AI ARR more than 8x YoY, Avarra contracted ARR +698%, and Alice's AI business +500% over two years. Few of these state a dollar ARR. https://fintech.global/2026/09/22/go-ai-raises-85m-series-a-for-on-prem-ai-push/ ; https://www.arr.club/avarra/avarra-grew-arr-400pct-yoy-raised-17m-series-a ; https://pulse2.com/alice-raises-140-million-as-ai-security-business-approaches-100-million-arr-and-grows-500/
- Big-tech Q3 AI user metrics are not out yet. Alphabet and Microsoft report later this week. SpaceX's Q3 call (Oct 20) gave Cursor only as period revenue: $608M for Aug 14-Sep 30, not an ARR, so it is not recorded. https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/20/spacex-spcx-earnings-live-updates-q3-2026html.html
