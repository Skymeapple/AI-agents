## Trends
- AI infrastructure for custom/open models keeps posting the fastest revenue ramps: Applied Compute $100M run rate in under 18 months, Fal at a $16B valuation on $800M+ annualized revenue.
- Public cloud and SaaS companies report AI as a customer-segment ARR (DigitalOcean AI Customer ARR +288%) or as agent counts (Freshworks), rather than as AI-product user numbers.
- Agent-security consolidation continues (CrowdStrike buying Fiddler AI and Dreadnode after Pangea).
