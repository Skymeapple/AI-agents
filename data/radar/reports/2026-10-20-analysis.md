### 2a. Anomaly verdicts (flags in the top 25)

- **morluto/rea: suspect, likely inflated.**
  - The measured pace (+5,004/day) is about a sixth of the stated weekly rate,
    so either the stated week was a spike or the stated count is wrong.
  - Watchers are 0.35% of stars (1.2k of 340.9k), and forks are 25%
    ([page](https://github.com/morluto/rea)).
  - A fact-check site puts it at 234,477 stars on Oct 14 ([pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/23845/rea-reverse-engineer-anything-github-true-false), [snippet]): about 106k in 6 days.
  - Real activity exists (686 issues, 605 PRs on the page), so the project is
    real; the star count is not credible as organic demand.
  - Its signal weight stays halved.
- **corrinehu/dsh-workbuddy-connect: launch spike, low confidence.**
  - It stated +904 in 7 days on 1,429 total; the radar measured +55 in the
    last day (1,429 → 1,484).
  - It has 5 watchers for 1.5k stars ([page](https://github.com/corrinehu/dsh-workbuddy-connect)).
  - Too small to call farming without contributor data.
- **Not flagged, but suspect: YuJunZhiXue/dsh-purge.**
  - Its growth was measured, not stated: +1,342 in a day.
  - But it has 18 watchers for 10.0k stars, and the README solicits stars
    ([page](https://github.com/YuJunZhiXue/dsh-purge)).
  - The radar's anomaly rule only checks stated gains against totals, so it
    cannot catch this. A watcher/fork ratio check would (backlog: star-farming
    check).

## 4. Innovation opportunities

The radar's hypotheses, each tested by research:

- **"Open source ahead of product: security": disproved as stated.** Commercial
  runtime-guardrail vendors exist and have been consolidated:
  - Check Point bought Lakera ([CSO](https://www.csoonline.com/article/4058653/check-point-acquires-lakera-to-build-a-unified-ai-security-stack.html), [snippet])
  - SentinelOne bought Prompt Security ([decryptiondigest](https://www.decryptiondigest.com/blog/ai-guardrail-vendors-after-acquisition-lakera-prompt-security-aim-calypsoai), [snippet])
  - Palo Alto bought Protect AI ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/palo-alto-networks-buy-protect-121905792.html), [snippet])

  **The narrower gap is real.** The open leaders (Cloudflare's skill, iFixAi)
  audit code and agents *from inside the coding agent*. The vendors guard
  production runtimes. Building there makes sense if teams will pay for audit
  findings inside their coding agent rather than as a separate scanner;
  iFixAi's paid Pro tier is the test case
  ([page](https://github.com/ifixai-ai/iFixAi)).
- **llm-gateway: disproved, crowded.** OpenRouter is valued at $1.3B
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/26/openrouter-more-than-doubles-valuation-to-1-3b-in-a-year), [snippet]), Vercel and Cloudflare offer gateways, and
  consolidation is under way. The open leaders here are "free API" proxies
  (freellmapi, free-claude-code), which reflects demand for free access, not a
  product gap.
- **agent-memory: disproved.**
  - Mem0 raised $24M and is AWS Agent SDK's memory provider ([pulse2](https://pulse2.com/mem0-24-million-raised-for-powering-memory-layer-for-ai-agents/), [snippet])
  - Letta and Zep are funded and charging ([agenticwire](https://www.agenticwire.news/article/mem0-zep-letta-agent-memory), [snippet])

  The radar missed them only because no collector tracks those products.
- **design: partly holds.** Napkin AI, Eraser and Excalidraw exist
  ([maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/napkin-ai-emerges-from-stealth-with-10m-funding-for-ai-tool-that-generates-business-visuals), [snippet]). No funded vendor ships diagram skills that run
  inside coding agents. But diagram-design shows how cheap the skill is to
  copy, so it is a weak moat.
- **finance: mixed.** Funded AI research tools for finance already exist (Rogo,
  Hebbia: [cryptobriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/rogo-arr-triples-50m-surpasses-hebbia/), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/hebbia/financials), [snippet]). No funded company sells
  price time-series foundation models, and Kronos's own demo warns against
  trading on it ([botmonster](https://botmonster.com/ai/kronos-financial-forecasting-model-bitcoin-24-hours/), [snippet]). The gap exists; whether it can be monetized is unproven.
- **agent-orchestration: disproved, crowded.** LangChain is at a $1.25B
  valuation ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/langchain), [snippet]); CrewAI, Trigger.dev and Inngest are funded.
- **local-llm: not researched today.**

**Two hypotheses of the radar agent's own:**

- *(radar agent's hypothesis)* **Trust and ranking for agent plugin
  marketplaces.**
  - Vendor marketplaces are trending: claude-plugins-official gained +5,441
    stars in 7 days ([weekly](https://github.com/trending?since=weekly)), and cursor/plugins is tracked too.
  - Meanwhile the star counts of skills and plugins are visibly manipulated
    (rea, dsh-purge).
  - So independent signals of which skills and plugins are safe and actually
    used (install telemetry, audit results) are scarce. It is worth building if
    marketplaces don't build this themselves.
- *(radar agent's hypothesis)* **Local voice for agents.**
  - VoiceStudio grew by adding MCP plus Claude Code, Cursor and Codex hooks to
    local TTS ([releases](https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio/releases)).
  - Anam reports 14x ARR growth in 13 months in real-time voice and video
    avatars (search-result summary, see the [inbox notes](../inbox/archive/2026-10-20-reported-growth.notes.md)).
  - Voice as an agent output channel, local and agent-wired, is underserved by
    the cloud TTS vendors.

## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Still all GitHub and company press.**
  - Of 86 tracked apps, every open-source signal came from GitHub pages read
    through WebFetch.
  - Hugging Face and Hacker News failed for the second day (DNS ENOTFOUND;
    Tavily over quota), and all four API collectors were refused (403).
  - Every one of today's 24 company figures came from search-result snippets,
    because no source page could be opened.
- **Re-observation gives only rounded counts.** Repo pages show "58.7k". The
  fix shipped today stops those from beating exact trending counts. But repos
  that are not on a trending page now resolve to only 100 stars. Since every
  re-observed repo was also on a trending page today, every delta in the report
  is exact.
- **Definitions are mixed within a metric.** `users` mixes WAU, MAU and web
  uniques, and revenue mixes gross and net. That produces deltas such as
  ChatGPT −$20B ($70B gross on Sep 29 vs $50B net on Sep 30) and Claude users
  245M → 111M. These are artefacts and should be ignored. Added to the backlog.
- **Rate-change signals are noisy.** Kronos ranks #8 on a 1-day-gain rate
  change (355 → 363). Added to the backlog.
- **Scorecard:** no forecast has matured; the first is expected about
  2026-10-27. The tuner stayed put (0 of the 3 required matured days).
- **Single change that would most improve tomorrow:** allow `huggingface.co`,
  `news.ycombinator.com`, `hn.algolia.com`, `api.github.com` and
  `api.npmjs.org` in the environment's network settings. That is a person's
  decision. Without it the radar sees only GitHub trending and the press.
