## 3. Directions

- **Steady leaders:** security (80% of its repos in the top quartile),
  image-generation (75%), agent-memory (67%).
- **Accelerating:** agent-orchestration (+0.09), on orca's release run.
- **Cooling:** finance (−0.09). Kronos's earlier lead rested on a rate-change
  signal that today's fix (§5) no longer counts.
- **Emergent groupings the tags don't name:**
  - *Agent-skill collections* ("agentic / optimization / control": android/skills,
    vercel-labs/skills, agentic-awesome-skills, scientific-agent-skills, ECC).
    Platform vendors (Google, Vercel, Anthropic) now publish official skill
    packs.
  - *Open dubbing and voice cloning* ("open-source / dubbing / cloning":
    voice-pro, VoiceStudio, KittenTTS, OpenCreator). Local voice now has four
    tracked projects.
- **Platform risk for unofficial clients is real.** dsh-workbuddy-connect shut
  down after its platform restricted third-party clients
  ([page](https://github.com/corrinehu/dsh-workbuddy-connect)). That is a
  standing risk for the "free API" gateway projects (freellmapi,
  free-claude-code) the llm-gateway direction is built on.

## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **"Fast and uncrowded / open ahead of product: generative-ui": disproved.**
  Commercial players are already in place:
  - CopilotKit: $27M Series A, a paid enterprise product
    ([pulse2](https://pulse2.com/copilotkit-27-million-series-a-raised-for-enterprise-agentic-frontend-stack/), [snippet]).
  - Thesys: commercial C1 API, $4M seed
    ([Entrepreneur](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/thesys-raises-usd-4-mn-funding-to-revolutionise-ai-user/482526), [snippet]).
  - Google: A2UI v0.9 ([Google](https://developers.googleblog.com/a2ui-v0-9-generative-ui/), [snippet]).
  - Vercel: v0 ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/vercel), [snippet]).

  thesysdev/openui and OpenIntelligentUI are those companies' own open funnels
  ([openui](https://github.com/thesysdev/openui), [OpenIntelligentUI](https://github.com/CopilotKit/OpenIntelligentUI)). The real contest is between
  protocols (AG-UI vs A2UI). No revenue specific to generative UI is published
  anywhere found, so whether it can be monetized is unproven.
- **agent-memory and agent-orchestration: disproved yesterday, and still
  disproved.** Mem0, Letta and Zep in memory; LangChain and CrewAI in
  orchestration (see [2026-10-20 analysis](2026-10-20-analysis.md) §4). The
  radar regenerates these because it tracks none of those companies (§5).
- **llm-gateway: disproved yesterday** (OpenRouter and others), and weaker
  today: the open leaders depend on access their platforms can revoke (§3).
- **Two of the radar agent's own:**
  - *(radar agent's hypothesis)* **Prompt-injection hardening for agent apps
    that act on untrusted content.** ai-job-search's fastest-growing week
    coincides with commits guarding job postings against prompt injection
    ([commits](https://github.com/MadsLorentzen/ai-job-search/commits/master)). Pluto Security just raised $42M for
    security around AI-built apps ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/13/pluto-security-raises-42m-as-ai-gives-enterprise-security-teams-a-new-headache/), [snippet]). A drop-in guard for
    skills that read email, job posts or web pages is worth building if skill
    authors keep hand-rolling one.
  - *(radar agent's hypothesis, carried from yesterday)* **Trust and ranking
    for skill and plugin marketplaces.** The case strengthened today: a
    star-soliciting top repo vanished, an unofficial client was shut down, and
    GitHub hid stargazer lists. Independent usage and safety signals for skills
    remain scarce.

## 5. Radar self-critique

  days apart. Without it, Kronos's daily gains moving from 355 to 363 stars
  counted as +16.9%/wk, which was yesterday's #8 app's strongest signal.
- **Deleted repos keep ranking.** dsh-purge is #3 on signals from before it
  vanished. The radar has no notion of "gone". Added to the backlog.
- **Hypotheses forget research.** The rule engine re-proposed agent-memory and
  agent-orchestration, both disproved yesterday, because the commercial
  incumbents aren't tracked apps. Added to the backlog: either add the
  incumbents as tracked apps, or keep a list of research verdicts the
  hypotheses check.
- **Sources are still narrow.**
  - All open-source data came from GitHub pages via WebFetch.
  - Hugging Face and HN failed for the third day; DNS fails even for mirrors.

  to 100. They mattered only for repos missing from trending, and none were
  missing today.
