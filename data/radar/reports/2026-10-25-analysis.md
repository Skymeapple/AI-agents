## 3. Directions

- **Security keeps the top slot**, carried by Cloudflare's skill and rea.
  - Commercially, AI-security vendors report growth multiples more often than
    dollars: HiddenLayer at least 10x over a year
    ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/?p=842675), [snippet]); Horizon3
    120%+.
- **Official vendor agent kits:** AWS's toolkit, Google's gemini-cli,
  Anthropic's claude-plugins-official and Cursor's plugins are all tracked.
  Platform vendors now ship skills and plugins for coding agents themselves,
  which narrows the space for third-party integrations.
- **Vertical agents scale fast** (all [snippet]):
  - Chamelio (legal): 4x in five months ([techstartups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/23/chamelio-raises-26m-series-a-for-ai-legal-agents-as-arr-quadruples-in-five-months/))

  - Owner (restaurants): over $100M ARR ([Goldman Sachs AM](https://am.gs.com/en-au/advisors/news/press-release/2026/owner-raises-240m-gs-alts))
- **Lab revenue framing stays contested.** One report has Anthropic "on pace"

## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **All 7 rule-generated hypotheses already carry research verdicts:**
  agent-orchestration, generative-ui, llm-gateway (×2 each) and agent-memory,
  all disproved. None was re-researched.
- **Two of the radar agent's own:**
  - *(radar agent's hypothesis, new)* **Independent quality checks for
    official vendor skills.** AWS, Google, Anthropic and Cursor now ship
    official skills and plugins for coding agents
    ([AWS toolkit](https://github.com/aws/agent-toolkit-for-aws)). Blacksmith's
    17x growth checking AI-written code shows teams pay for verification
    (above). A neutral test harness that scores vendor skills on real tasks
    (correctness, cost, safety) is the gap. It is worth building if teams mix
    vendors' skills and need to choose between them.
  - *(carried)* **Guardrailed packaging for offensive-security skills.**
    Unchanged since Oct 24. Horizon3's confirmed $100M ARR strengthens the
    demand side.
