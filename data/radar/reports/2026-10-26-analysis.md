## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **All 6 rule-generated hypotheses already carry research verdicts**
  (disproved earlier this week). None was re-researched.
- **Two of the radar agent's own:**
  - *(radar agent's hypothesis, new)* **Design review as a service for
    agent-built frontends.** Three anti-slop design skills are among the
    fastest open movers (above). They are free, prompt-level and easy to copy.
    The gap is the step after: automated visual QA that scores a deployed UI
    against a brand system and blocks a merge. It is worth building if teams
    ship agent-written frontends at volume and the skills alone don't hold
    quality.
  - *(carried)* **Independent quality checks for official vendor skills.** It
    strengthened today: Microsoft and JetBrains joined AWS, Google and
    Anthropic with official agent tooling.
