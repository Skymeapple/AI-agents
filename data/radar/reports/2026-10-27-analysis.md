## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **All 5 rule-generated hypotheses already carry research verdicts.** None
  was re-researched.
- **Two of the radar agent's own:**
  - *(radar agent's hypothesis, revised)* **Third-party agent-skill
    verification.**
    - The original framing was "independent quality checks for vendor
      skills". It is now partly pre-empted: NVIDIA ships SkillSpector and a
      Verified Skills pipeline ([page](https://github.com/NVIDIA/SkillSpector)),
      and Anthropic ships sandbox-runtime.
    - What remains open: verification that is neither the skill's vendor nor
      the agent's vendor. Marketplaces whose skills come from many vendors
      would need it.
    - Worth building only if a neutral marketplace emerges. Platform-owner
      tools are free and will be the default.
  - *(carried)* **Design review as a service for agent-built frontends.**
    Strengthened by hallmark's and impeccable's continued acceleration.
