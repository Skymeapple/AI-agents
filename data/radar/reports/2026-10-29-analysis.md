## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Improvement made today: a `basis` field for users and revenue levels.**
  - `users` can be marked dau, wau, mau, registered, paid_seats or
    web_uniques; revenue run-rates gross, net or contracted.
  - Levels on different bases are never differenced into growth, so a
    company switching from MAU to "users" can't produce a fake jump.
  - The inbox loader rejects a basis that doesn't fit the metric.
  - No row today stated a basis clearly enough to set one. Gemini's "users"
    was left unmarked.
- **Two rows dropped before ingest:**
  - Microsoft's "more than 5x" YoY Copilot daily users describes the same
    growth as the "nearly 50%" QoQ figure; only the latest quarter is kept.
  - Databricks' "+80% in the second quarter" carried a Jul 31 date the
    collector inferred; the source didn't state it.
