### 2a. Anomaly verdicts

- **Brand-new API repos** still carry the "gained all its stars this week"
  flag, with yesterday's reviews beside it: deepseek organic; unslop, Mika
  and pixelfx unclear. Their second day of measured growth
  (+1.1k to +1.3k each) argues against a one-day spike.
- **dsh-our-free-model: not judged, but a risk to flag.** "Free, unlimited"
  access to paid frontier models through a plugin needs someone paying for
  inference. The usual answers are shared or leaked keys, or a reseller.
  Nothing on the page explains it. Its stars may be real; what it offers
  deserves scepticism before anyone builds on it.
- **cleanyourtv** stays an off-topic fast grower (§2 #7).

## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **Rule hypotheses: 7, down from 83.** New today and researched:
  - **cad, "open source ahead of product": disproved.** Zoo ships Design
    Studio with Text-to-CAD and, since March, the Zookeeper CAD agent
    ([Digital Engineering](https://www.digitalengineering247.com/article/zoo-introduces-zookeeper-agent-for-cad-design)).
  - **cad, "fast and uncrowded": partly disproved.** Zoo and Adam, a YC
    company that raised $4.1M for a CAD copilot
    ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=3063885)), are small, funded
    players. The open repos lead on attention, not on product.
  - **jailbreak (both rules): disproved as an opportunity.** Jailbreak
    libraries are misuse tooling. The defensive side, red-teaming and agent
    audits, is served by Alice, HiddenLayer and iFixAi on the radar.
  - **context-compression, "open source ahead of product":** not researched
    today.
- *(radar agent's hypothesis, new)* **Plugin trust for agent harnesses.**
  The dsh ecosystem shows what happens when a harness gets a plugin market.
  Within weeks it has a jailbreak plugin and a "free models" plugin of
  unclear provenance. A scanner or signed registry for harness plugins
  would extend what NVIDIA SkillSpector does for skills, for a market
  growing faster than skills did. It is worth building if DeepSeek or
  another harness owner doesn't ship it first.
