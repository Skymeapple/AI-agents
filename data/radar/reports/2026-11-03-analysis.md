## 3. Directions

- **Computer-use agents are the new wave:** vercel-labs/agent-desktop (a
  computer-use CLI), OpenMausBot (bots with their own cloud computers),
  Mika (an Android agent) and phone-harness ("let your agent control your
  phone"). The radar has no `computer-use` direction. It shows up so far
  in emergent groupings and raw topics; if it holds for a few more days it
  should join the vocabulary.
- **Offensive-security agents:** KeygraphHQ/shannon (51k) runs real
  exploits. With Claude-Red and iFixAi, AI-run pentesting is now a crowded
  open field. It is a real commercial opportunity (§4).
- **Model "uncensoring" tools** (p-e-w/heretic) join the jailbreak libraries.
  The jailbreak direction now has 3 apps and carries Nov 2's verdict (not an
  opportunity).

## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **Rule hypotheses: 7, all carrying verdicts except context-compression.**
  That one stays unresearched; claude-mem and billion-context lead it. No
  new rules fired.
- *(radar agent's hypothesis, carried)* **Plugin trust for agent harnesses**
  (from Nov 2). Strengthened: dsh-our-free-model accelerated to +1,639 a
  day, and its business model is still unexplained.
- *(radar agent's hypothesis, new)* **Continuous AI pentesting as a
  service.** shannon and Claude-Red show the demand. What open repos don't
  give a security team is safe, scoped, audited runs against production,
  with reports a compliance team accepts. Commercial AI-security vendors
  are on the radar (Horizon3.ai NodeZero, HiddenLayer, Alice). The gap is
  agent-native web/API pentesting priced for small teams. It is worth
  building only if exploit runs can be sandboxed convincingly.

## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Improvement made today: apps can be marked out of scope.**
  - `airadar mark-out-of-scope <url> <page> "<what it is>"` writes to an
    append-only file (`--back` to undo; it follows repo moves). The app
    leaves the ranking, directions, movers and recheck list, and its
    observations stay. The report lists them.
  - First use: 50 apps. I read the descriptions of the 55 repos that came
    in only through the generic search terms. Six turned out to be AI
    despite no AI keyword (two dsh plugins, an ML pretraining repo, an ML
    paper, a Hermes agent kit and a World Labs tool) and stayed in scope.
    So did android-ad-cleaner, which advertises AI-agent execution. That
    left 49, plus cleanyourtv (an Android TV debloater, #2 yesterday).
- **Correction to a collector instruction.** I told the growth collector
  not to convert month windows into days. It read that as also excluding
  "year-over-year", and left out Cohere's +165%. A year-over-year figure
  states its window, and earlier runs recorded such figures at 365 days, so
  I added it before ingest with a note.
- **The scorecard keeps sliding** (40%, 33%, 40% across Oct 25–27). These
  were the last forecasts made almost entirely from page reads. The tuner,
  over 9 days, now finds two small edges and adopts neither:
