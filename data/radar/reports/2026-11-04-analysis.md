## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **agent-skills, "open source ahead of product": partly disproved.**
  - Platform owners publish skills free:
    [anthropics/skills](https://github.com/anthropics/skills),
    [vercel-labs/skills](https://github.com/vercel-labs/skills) and
    [android/skills](https://github.com/android/skills).
  - So a skill itself is not a product gap. The open commercial angle is
    distribution and trust: marketplaces and verification. NVIDIA's
    SkillSpector already covers part of that.
  - Verdict recorded.
- *(radar agent's hypothesis, carried)* **Plugin trust for agent harnesses.**
  dsh-our-free-model is at 59,566 stars and still accelerating. Its business
  model is still unexplained.
- *(radar agent's hypothesis, new)* **Sandboxed computer use for agents.**
  agent-desktop, phone-harness and Mika give agents control of a real
  desktop or phone. Running that safely needs per-session machines,
  permission prompts and audit trails. That is the layer OpenMausBot's
  "own cloud computer" gestures at. Worth building if agent-desktop's
  adoption continues for a week, before Vercel or another platform owner
  bundles it.
