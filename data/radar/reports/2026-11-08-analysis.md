## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **Rule hypotheses: 7, all with verdicts except context-compression.**
- *(radar agent's hypothesis, new)* **Trust signals for repos that agents
  install.**
  - The rpocket campaign shows how easily stars are farmed for lure repos.
  - Coding agents increasingly install skills, plugins and tools straight
    from GitHub.
  - A reputation check (account age, fork/watcher ratios, near-duplicate
    naming across accounts, deletion history) before an agent installs
    anything would catch exactly this pattern.
  - Related to NVIDIA SkillSpector (content scanning). The gap is
    provenance and popularity signals. Worth building if agent platforms
    don't add it to their skill and plugin installers.
- *(carried)* Sandboxed desktop/phone control for agents; plugin trust for
  agent harnesses.

## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Improvement made today: search terms are mined only from on-topic
  groupings.**
  - A grouping now yields search terms only if most of its members carry
    one of the radar's direction tags. Fake-repo clusters score high and
    have distinctive words, but their tags (e.g. `mc-cheat-client`) are
    off-topic.
  - The automatic terms were reset to the three mined before the API ran:
    autonomous, skill, codex. 18 junk terms were dropped.
  - A test pins it.
- **I missed this for a week.** The bad terms accumulated from Nov 1, and
  I didn't look at the term list until today, when the recheck list filled
  with deleted fakes. The backlog now asks for the report to list each
  auto term and what it brought in, so a bad term shows the day it's mined.
- **The scorecard's new scale.** From Nov 1 the forecasts are compared on
  hundreds of apps rather than dozens. Precision@15 will read lower even as
  the radar gets better; chance-adjusted, the Nov 1 forecast is the best
  yet. Tuning over 14 days: relative_weight → 0.4 is +0.005 (better on 64%
  of days), below the bars.

  reported only secondhand (FourWeekMBA citing IT Home) and not
  corroborated.
