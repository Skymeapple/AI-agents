## 3. Directions

- **Agent harnesses are a mass-market product.** DeepSeek Harness's 1.54M
  daily users are a scale no other open-source agent tool on the radar
  has reported. Its plugin ecosystem (dsh-Go, dsh-our-free-model,
  ds-harness-remote) keeps growing.
- **Design systems for agents:** meta/astryx and google/elephant-goldfish-model
  both accelerated. Platform owners are shipping the design layer agents
  build against.
- **Defending software against AI agents:** qybaihe/bla (shielding
  binaries from LLM analysis) is a counterpart to the agent-security tools.
