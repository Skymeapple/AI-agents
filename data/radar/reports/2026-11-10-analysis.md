## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Improvement made today: a like-for-like scorecard figure.**
  - The scorecard (report, dashboard CSV and shared page) now shows,
    beside the full Spearman, the Spearman over the forecast's own top 100
    apps among those compared.
  - It is a fixed-size pool, so days whose pools differ tenfold can be
    compared. It is reported only: the tuner and the yardstick are
    unchanged.
  - A test pins it: padding a pool with 900 easy-to-rank apps lifts the
    full figure above 0.5 while the top-100 figure stays at −1.
- **What it shows is uncomfortable.** On the like-for-like figure the
  forecasts got worse when the pool grew, so I withdraw yesterday's claim.
  - **A plausible cause:** the forecast's top 100 now includes many new
    repos scored on one stated API gain. Those can surge or stall.
  - **To test next:** compare the top-100 figure for apps with two or
    more readings against apps with one.
- **The tuner optimises the inflated figure.** Its backtests average the
  full Spearman, so a setting that sorts the padding better can win.
  - **Needs a person:** should the tuner judge candidates on the
    like-for-like figure?
  - It changes what the radar optimises. The guardrails don't forbid it:
    the yardstick (`realised_scores`) stays fixed, and the thresholds and
    bounds are untouched. But it is a change to the tuner's objective, so
    I have not made it.
