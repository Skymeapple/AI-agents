## 3. Directions

- **Platforms are shipping the agent toolkit themselves.** Among this
  week's fastest growers:
  - Vercel: ui and agent-desktop
  - Google Chrome: modern-web-guidance
  - OpenAI: codex-security
  - Cloudflare: security-audit-skill
  - Jane Street: agent_skills
  - Alibaba: open-code-review

  Independent skill and agent-tool repos now compete with free ones from
  the platforms their users already depend on.
- **Agent skills as a vertical product:** finance (claude-trading-skills,
  day-trader-skill) and social media (instagram-agent-skill) skills are
  trending. Skills are being packaged for specific jobs, not just coding.
- **Generative UI and design for agents:** meta/astryx is cooling as
  vercel-labs/ui rises. Same direction (design / generative-ui), and a
  second platform owner has entered it.

## 4. Innovation opportunities

- **Rule hypotheses:** 5 fire today, all carrying verdicts.
- *(mine, carried)* **Plugin trust for agent harnesses**; **like and star
  provenance for model hubs** (Nov 13).
- *(mine, new)* **A curation layer over platform-issued skills.** With
  Vercel, Google, OpenAI, Cloudflare and Jane Street all publishing skills,
  the open question is which to install, not who makes them.
  - Likely incumbents, not yet checked: the platforms' own skill
    directories and plugin catalogues.
  - What would have to be true: teams install skills from several
    platforms, and none of the platforms indexes the others.
  - Not researched yet.
