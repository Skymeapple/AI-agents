## 3. Directions

- **Fake-repo campaigns copy AI tags.** The rpocket/resocket campaign
  (Nov 8) used game-cheat names. Today's "ai--manus" campaign named itself
  after a real AI agent product and carried direction tags. Tag-based
  filters (the on-topic check for mining, the intake filter) do not catch
  that. What did catch it: the same repo name under many fresh-looking
  owners, and mass deletion.
- **Platform agent tooling**: Google Chrome (guidance plus DevTools MCP),
  Vercel (ui), Tencent (LoopForge), NVIDIA (skills), Alibaba
  (open-code-review). The week's pattern holds.
- **Frontier-lab revenue:** Anthropic's filed $81B run rate is the most
  authoritative revenue figure the radar holds for any AI company.

## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Improvement made today: owner-name fragments are no longer mined.**
  - Pieces of hyphenated owner names ("gentleman" from
    Gentleman-Programming, "daslab" from ISTA-DASLab) now count as owner
    names when filtering mined terms, unless they are known vocabulary.
  - Checked on the live registry: it blocks both and none of the
    productive terms (skill, codex, manus, gguf).
  - The first test I wrote passed vacuously because no group formed.
    It was rebuilt so the group forms and the fragments are shown to
    appear without the fix.
  - The two existing fragments moved to the drop list.
- **Generic words** ("give", "upstream") were caught by the intake filter
  today (19 of 69 and 5 of 46 repos tagged) and dropped. New mined terms:
  "github-copilot-app", "steering", "text-to-image".
- **New gap: same-name clone campaigns.** Added to the backlog: flag a
  repo name that appears under many owners created recently, before it
  reaches the ranking.
- **Reported figures can conflict.** Anthropic's $100B (press) vs $81B
  (filing) shows the radar needs a way to mark a reading as superseded by
  a better source without deleting it. Added to the backlog.
- **Sources:**
  - Tavily extract read all three reported figures, including a PDF
    filing, and served as a bulk 404 checker for 41 GitHub pages in three
    calls.
  - Hacker News and npm are still refused by the proxy.
