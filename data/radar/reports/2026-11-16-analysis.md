## 3. Directions

- **Agent skills: platforms and verticals.** Google Labs (stitch-skills),
  NVIDIA (SkillEvaluator, skills), Jane Street, Anthropic. NVIDIA shipping
  an evaluator for skills suggests the next layer is quality control, not
  more skills.
- **AI inside creative and office tools:** univer (office suite SDK) and
  blender-ai-mcp-addon (3D) both grew fast.
- **Fake repos are now an industry.** Five campaigns in the registry,
  hundreds of accounts, stars bought in batches of identical counts. Every
  one borrowed the name of something real and in demand: AI Manus, the
  GitHub Copilot app, access to Gemini.

## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Improvement made today: clone-campaign detection.**
  - `clone_campaigns()` flags a repo name shared by at least 5 owners, all
    first seen within 7 days, with at least 60% of members tying another
    member's star count to within 2%.
  - On today's registry it separates cleanly: campaigns 92–100% twins,
    real shared names ("skills") 11%.
  - My first version used star spread and missed three campaigns that bought
    stars in several batches; the twin share catches them.
  - It is report-only: the report lists the groups, and marking stays a
    reviewed step. Today's review kept the genuine original.
  - A test pins it (a campaign flagged; six real "skills" repos not).
- **Still open:**
  - The check runs after a term has already pulled clones in. Feeding it
    back into mining (drop a term whose intake is mostly flagged clones)
    is the natural next step.
  - New mined terms today: "ai-video", "reverse-engineer", "tailored".
- **Forecasts:** Nov 9 scored 0.44 / 20%. The like-for-like figure has
  hovered at 0.40–0.53 since Nov 3.
- **Sources:** Hacker News and npm are still refused by the proxy. Tavily
  extract handled the reported figures and the 404 checks.
