## 1. Summary

- **Disproved: short-span deltas are not what hold the forecasts back.**
  The planned backtest re-scored every matured forecast with measured
  deltas restricted to spans of at least 3 or 7 days. Means over the
  API-era forecasts (Nov 1–11):

  | Minimum span | Full Spearman | Spearman, top 100 | Top-15 hit rate |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | none (current) | 0.833 | 0.398 | 32% |
  | 3 days | 0.766 | 0.384 | 27% |
  | 7 days | 0.696 | 0.353 | 23% |

  - Every restriction is worse on every measure, in October too. Short
    deltas carry real signal, and dropping them only shrinks the pool
    (2,217 → 1,405 apps on Nov 11).
  - No setting changes; the candidates are worse, not better.
  - The like-for-like problem is elsewhere. Today's newly matured
    forecasts were among the weakest: Nov 10 scored 0.31 and Nov 11
    0.23 on their top 100.
- **A star purge caught the radar out.**
  [hitkeeen/blender-ai-mcp-addon](https://github.com/hitkeeen/blender-ai-mcp-addon)
  read 1,811 stars on Nov 16 [API]; today its page shows 34 stars, 0

  - Lesson: issue counts can be faked as cheaply as stars.

## 5. Radar self-critique

- **Improvement made today: the span backtest.**
  - `extract_signals` has a diagnostic `min_delta_span_days` switch
    (default 0: unchanged).
  - `span_backtest()` re-scores every matured forecast at minimum spans
    0, 3 and 7, reporting the full and like-for-like scores. A test pins
    the switch.
  - Result above: disproved, so nothing is adopted.
  - The yardstick and the tuner are untouched.
- **What's left to explain the weak like-for-like score:** the top 100 is
  increasingly made of new, fast-starting repos, some with bought stars
  that are later purged. A purged repo's realised growth collapses after
  the forecast, so the forecast looks wrong. Next test: re-score the
  like-for-like figure excluding apps later found gone, out of scope or
  reviewed inorganic.
- **The 2-day gap:** handled; the recheck list needed a second
  `collect --only github-repos` pass (max 3 passes per run was not enough
  after two days).
- **Sources:** Hacker News and npm are still refused by the proxy. Tencent
  Q3 was not out at research time.
