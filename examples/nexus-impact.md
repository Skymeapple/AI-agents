# Research directions priced by world impact

_Generated by `python -m nexus.cli impact` from `demo_corpus.json`; 4,000 paired draws, seed 20261128, as of 2026-11-28._

## Read this first

- **The corpus is synthetic.** Every work in it is machine-generated, so the directions, their success rates and their arrival dates describe no real field. This page shows the method working end to end; it is not a finding about any technology.
- **The lever mapping is assumed.** 7 of the 7 values in `nexus/impact_map.yaml` are judgement calls with no data behind them, including how much of a lever one technology is worth. Every energy direction is given the same size, so differences between them below come only from sciscout's success rate and timing.
- **worldsim is not a forecast.** Most of its links and all of its lever effects are assumed; its own report says so.
- **Late arrival is approximated.** worldsim holds a lever constant from 2025 to 2050, so a direction arriving later is given the constant lever with the same lever-years. For outcomes marked `exact` the lever's own term gives the same 2050 value either way; for those marked `approx` it does not, and for warming, where an early cut counts for more than a late one, it overstates the benefit.
