## Validation summary

* 12 pytest tests. They rebuild the LiteLLM, agent-framework (original and renamed files), Hades, planted-`_virtualenv.py` and PEP 829 `.start` attack shapes with inert payloads, and assert that scanned hooks are **never executed**.
* Real environments: the system Python plus 3 virtualenvs (pip + virtualenv, pip, uv; 800 MB+ of popular packages including numpy, pandas, scikit-learn, Django, FastAPI, Jupyter, boto3, OpenTelemetry, coverage, autowrapt, setuptools / hatch / uv editables). **Found and fixed one false positive:** coverage 7.17's own `a1_coverage.pth` uses `exec(...)` and was flagged critical; autowrapt's `.pth` and `.start` hooks were also flagged. After fixing, 10/10 real startup files were judged correctly, in under 0.2 s.
* Observation: an 801 MB environment of about 25 mainstream packages had **zero** `.pth` files. Executable startup hooks are rare, so each one deserves a look.
* The wheel builds (`pip wheel`), installs in a clean venv and runs. `python -S -m pthscan` works, so the scanner never processes `.pth` files itself.
