## 1. Gene editing: Casgevy uptake, base/prime editing, in vivo editing, personalized CRISPR and the plausible-mechanism pathway, epigenetic editing

### Takeaway
2026 is the year in vivo CRISPR moved from proof of concept to registration. Intellia's lonvo-z (HAE) had a positive Phase 3, and its BLA was accepted with priority review on Sep 8, 2026 (PDUFA Mar 10, 2027). Lilly/Verve's one-shot PCSK9 base editor was published in NEJM with LDL-C cuts of up to 62%. The FDA formalized a "plausible mechanism" route for bespoke editors after the baby KJ case. Ex vivo Casgevy is growing fast but from a small base, at $76M in Q2 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Casgevy (ex vivo, Vertex/CRISPR Tx)**
- Q2 2026 Casgevy revenue was **$76M**. That is +78% quarter over quarter and +151% year over year vs Q2 2025. Vertex said there were more infusions in H1 2026 than in all of 2025, helped by expanded pediatric approvals and reimbursement agreements (Aug 3, 2026) — [Vertex Q2 2026 release](https://news.vrtx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/vertex-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results); [Yahoo/GuruFocus call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/vertex-pharmaceuticals-inc-vrtx-q2-050826035.html)
- Vertex/CRISPR projected "nearly 3x" Casgevy growth for 2026 (Feb 2026 guidance commentary; this is a projection) — [PackGene summary](https://www.packgene.com/frontier/021326-vertex-and-crispr/); [BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/business/vertex-crispr-set-lofty-goal-for-casgevy-gene-therapy-as-patient-starts-ramp)

**In vivo CRISPR nuclease (Intellia)**
- **Lonvo-z (HAE, KLKB1 knockout)**: in Phase 3 HAELO, **62%** of lonvo-z patients were attack-free and free of HAE therapy over the 6-month efficacy period, vs **11%** on placebo. No SAEs were seen in the lonvo-z arm. The **BLA was accepted with Priority Review on Sep 8, 2026, with PDUFA Mar 10, 2027**. If approved, it would be the first in vivo CRISPR therapy approved anywhere — [Intellia PR, Sep 8 2026](https://ir.intelliatx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/intellia-therapeutics-announces-fda-acceptance-biologics-license); [BioPharma Dive on Phase 3](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/intellia-gene-editing-hereditary-angioedema-lonvo-z-results/818503/); rolling BLA began in April 2026 — [Big Molecule Watch, Apr 28 2026](https://www.bigmoleculewatch.com/2026/04/28/intellia-initiates-rolling-bla-submission-for-in-vivo-crispr-therapy-in-hereditary-angioedema/)
- **Nex-z (TTR amyloidosis)**: the FDA placed clinical holds on **Oct 29, 2025** after a patient had Grade 4 transaminase elevation and raised bilirubin. The hold on MAGNITUDE-2 (ATTRv-PN) was lifted in **Jan 2026**, and the hold on MAGNITUDE (ATTR-CM) on **Mar 2, 2026** — [Intellia PR, Mar 2 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/03/02/3247267/0/en/intellia-therapeutics-announces-fda-lift-of-clinical-hold-on-magnitude-phase-3-clinical-trial-in-attr-cm.html); [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/intellia-fda-lifts-hold-nex-z-crispr-ttr-amyloidosis/810584/)

**In vivo base editing (Lilly/Verve)**
- **VERVE-102** (ABE mRNA plus a PCSK9 guide delivered by GalNAc-LNP) in the Heart-2 Phase 1b gave dose-dependent reductions of up to **88% in PCSK9** and up to **62% in LDL-C** with durable effect after one infusion. It was presented at EAS on **May 25, 2026** and published simultaneously in NEJM. Lilly acquired Verve in 2025 — [Lilly PR](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/single-dose-lillys-pcsk9-base-editor-verve-102-reduced-pcsk9-88); [NEJM](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa2601283); [TCTMD](https://www.tctmd.com/news/gene-editing-therapy-safely-lowers-pcsk9-ldl-cholesterol-phase-i-heart-2)

**Prime editing**
- Prime Medicine's **PM359** (ex vivo, chronic granulomatous disease) produced the first clinical prime-editing data in 2025, with rapid restoration of DHR positivity. The company says this supports accelerated approval — [Prime Medicine PR](https://investors.primemedicine.com/news-releases/news-release-details/prime-medicine-announces-breakthrough-clinical-data-showing); [Inside Precision Medicine](https://www.insideprecisionmedicine.com/topics/precision-medicine/prime-editing-clinical-results-published-for-the-first-time-ever/)
- In vivo prime editors in the pipeline: **PM577 (Wilson disease)** with filings in H1 2026 and a Phase 1/2 starting in H2 2026, and **PM647 (AATD)** with IND/CTA in Q3 2026. Initial data for both are expected in **2027** — [Prime Medicine 8-K filings, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001894562/000162828026053878/q220268-kex991xpressrelease.htm)

**Personalized CRISPR and the FDA pathway**
- **KJ Muldoon** (CPS1 deficiency) was the first person to receive a bespoke in vivo base editor. The first dose was Feb 25, 2025, and the therapy was designed, cleared and delivered in about 6 months. As of the March 2026 update he was hitting developmental milestones and walking — [CHOP one-year anniversary](https://www.chop.edu/news/childrens-hospital-philadelphia-marks-one-year-anniversary-worlds-first-personalized-crispr); [NUCDF, Mar 2 2026](https://nucdf.org/news.html/article/2026/03/02/rare-disease-day-at-nih-offers-update-on-kj-muldoon-future-plans-for-personalized-gene-editing-for-ucds)
- The CHOP/Penn team planned a **2026 umbrella Phase I/II IND covering 7 urea-cycle-disorder genes**. They argue that approval of the editing *platform* could rest on as few as **5-10 patients** — [Inside Precision Medicine](https://www.insideprecisionmedicine.com/topics/precision-medicine/in-vivo-gene-editing-for-rare-diseases-begins-with-umbrella-clinical-trial/); [STAT, Oct 16 2025](https://www.statnews.com/2025/10/16/baby-kj-crispr-gene-editing-personalized-medicine-at-scale/)
- **FDA "Plausible Mechanism Pathway"**: Makary and Prasad outlined it in NEJM (Nov 2025), and **draft guidance followed in Feb 2026**. It covers genome editing and RNA therapies (e.g., ASOs) for rare diseases with a known molecular cause. An FDA official later said the principles are not limited to bespoke gene therapies — [NPR, Feb 23 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/02/23/nx-s1-5720948/fda-rare-disease-gene-therapy); [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/fda-guidance-personalized-therapies-rare-diseases-hhs/812890/); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/fda-official-plausible-mechanism-principles-not-exclusive-bespoke-gene-therapies). Critics raised concerns about the evidentiary standard — [STAT, Jan 26 2026](https://www.statnews.com/2026/01/26/fda-makary-prasad-crispr-gene-editing-concerns-baby-kj/)

**Epigenetic editing**
- **Tune Therapeutics TUNE-401** (HBV epigenetic silencer that methylates integrated HBV DNA and cccDNA without cutting DNA) presented **positive Phase 1b/2a proof-of-concept data at EASL, May 30, 2026**. Tune describes this as the first clinical evidence of epigenetic silencing in HBV — [BusinessWire, May 30 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260530052195/en/Tune-Therapeutics-Presents-Positive-Phase-1b2a-Proof-of-Concept-Data-on-TUNE-401-a-First-in-Class-Epigenetic-Silencer-for-Patients-with-Hepatitis-B-at-EASL-2026); [Endpoints](https://endpoints.news/epigenetic-editing-shows-promise-against-chronic-hep-b-infections/)
- The IGI publishes an annual overview of CRISPR clinical trials — [IGI, CRISPR Clinical Trials: A 2026 Update](https://innovativegenomics.org/news/crispr-clinical-trials-2026/)
- MIT Technology Review's 2026 "10 Breakthrough Technologies" list (Jan 12, 2026) includes gene editing. The exact entry name was not retrieved — [MIT TR](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/01/12/1130697/10-breakthrough-technologies-2026/)

### Inferences (researcher estimates)
- **Lonvo-z FDA approval by Jun 30, 2027: ~80%.** Basis: priority review, a clean Phase 3 with no SAEs in the treated arm, and a PDUFA date of Mar 10, 2027. The main risk is CMC or liver-safety scrutiny carried over from nex-z. Leading indicators: an AdCom announcement (or none), label negotiation, and any late-cycle meeting news.
- **Nex-z MAGNITUDE (ATTR-CM) topline by end-2028: ~55%; approval by 2031: ~40%.** The hold cost months, the trial is event-driven, and it competes with tafamidis, acoramidis and silencers.
- **VERVE-102 entering Phase 2/3 by end-2027: ~70%.** A one-shot PCSK9 approval by 2031 is ~25%, because cardiovascular outcomes trials are long. A surrogate (LDL-C) path would need FDA agreement.
- **First approval under the plausible-mechanism framework (any ASO or editor) by end-2028: ~50%.** First approval of the CHOP UCD umbrella *platform* by 2030: ~35%.
- **First clinical in vivo prime-editing data by end-2027: ~65%** (PM577 Wilson).
- **Casgevy**: annualized H1 2026 revenue is about $250-300M. FY2026 of about $250-350M looks consistent with the "~3x" guide (inference). Global cumulative infusions above 1,000 by 2029 is plausible, but no patient-count figures were retrieved.

## 2. Obesity and metabolic: next-gen incretins, sales, prices, new indications, generics

### Takeaway
Incretins are now the largest drug class by revenue. Lilly's tirzepatide franchise was $14.9B in Q2 2026 alone, and Lilly guides $85-87B total revenue for 2026. Orals launched: the Wegovy pill was approved in Dec 2025, and Lilly's orforglipron (Foundayo) was approved in Apr 2026. Retatrutide's Phase 3 showed about 25-30% weight loss and filings are in 2026. Prices are falling sharply through MFN/TrumpRx deals, the Medicare $50 bridge, and Novo's cut of the Wegovy list price to $675 from Jan 2027. Semaglutide generics have started in India, Canada and elsewhere. Alzheimer's failed (EVOKE), while alcohol use disorder shows positive signals.

### Cited Findings
**Lilly Q2 2026 (reported Aug 5, 2026)**
- Revenue was **$23.0B (+48% y/y)**. Mounjaro plus Zepbound was **$14.9B** (+$6.3B y/y). Mounjaro worldwide was **$9.9B (+91%)**, of which US $4.8B (+45%). Zepbound was **$4.9B (+44%)**, partly offset by lower realized prices. **FY2026 revenue guidance was raised to $85-87B**, with non-GAAP EPS of $35.50-36.50 — [Lilly Q2 2026 release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-raises-full); [CNBC, Aug 5 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/eli-lilly-lly-earnings-q2-2026.html)

Lilly also submitted it for type 2 diabetes — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/05/eli-lilly-lly-earnings-q2-2026.html); [Yahoo call highlights](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/eli-lilly-co-lly-q2-210754282.html)

**Pipeline**

## 3. Cell therapy for autoimmunity, in vivo CAR-T, xenotransplantation, mRNA cancer vaccines, Alzheimer's, longevity

### Takeaway
Personalized mRNA cancer vaccines had their first positive Phase 3 (INTerpath-001, announced Aug 19, 2026). Pig-kidney xenotransplantation entered formal FDA-cleared trials: United Therapeutics' EXPAND (up to 50 patients) with its first transplant Nov 3, 2025, plus eGenesis. Autoimmune CAR-T is nearing its first BLA, Kyverna's miv-cel for stiff-person syndrome with the rolling BLA to complete in Q4 2026. In vivo CAR-T is in early human trials, including in lupus. Anti-amyloid Alzheimer's therapy is moving toward prevention (Roche trontinemab PrevenTRON), and blood tests are cleared but have quality problems.

### Cited Findings
**mRNA individualized neoantigen therapy**
- **INTerpath-001** (intismeran autogene plus Keytruda vs Keytruda, resected stage IIB-IV melanoma, n=**1,137**, randomized 2:1) **met RFS and DMFS endpoints (announced Aug 19, 2026)**. There were no new safety signals. It is the first positive Phase 3 for an individualized neoantigen and mRNA cancer therapy — [Merck PR](https://www.merck.com/news/merck-and-moderna-announce-phase-3-interpath-001-trial-of-intismeran-autogene-plus-keytruda-met-endpoints-of-recurrence-free-survival-rfs-and-distant-metastasis-free-survival-dmfs-in-patient/); [ASCO Post](https://ascopost.com/news/august-2026/interpath-001-trial-of-mrna-based-individualized-neoantigen-therapy-meets-primary-and-key-secondary-endpoints-in-patients-with-high-risk-resected-melanoma/)
- In the Phase 2b KEYNOTE-942, 5-year follow-up presented at **ASCO 2026** showed a **49%** reduction in recurrence or death and a **59%** reduction in distant metastasis or death vs pembrolizumab alone — [BioPharm International](https://www.biopharminternational.com/view/merck-moderna-intismeran-autogene-pembrolizumab-phase-3-melanoma)

**Xenotransplantation**
- **United Therapeutics EXPAND** (UKidney, 10 gene edits; ESRD patients unlikely to get a human kidney within 5 years). The first trial transplant was at **NYU Langone on Nov 3, 2025**, with up to **50 patients** planned — [NYU Langone](https://nyulangone.org/news/first-gene-edited-pig-kidney-transplant-clinical-trial-begins-nyu-langone-health); [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13525214/); FDA clearance was reported Sep 2025 — [CNN, Sep 8 2025](https://www.cnn.com/2025/09/08/health/pig-kidney-transplant-human-trial-fda)
- **eGenesis** (69-edit pig) received FDA clearance for a 3-patient study — [CRISPR Medicine News](https://crisprmedicinenews.com/news/egenesis-receives-fda-clearance-for-gene-edited-pig-kidney-trial-in-end-stage-kidney-disease-patient/)
- **Tim Andrews** (eGenesis kidney, Jan 2025) set a record of **271 days** with a functioning pig kidney before removal in Oct 2025. He received a human kidney in Jan 2026, the first xenograft-to-allograft bridge — [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13525214/)

**CAR-T for autoimmune disease**
- **Kyverna miv-cel (KYV-101, autologous CD19)**: positive registrational **KYSA-8 (stiff-person syndrome)** and positive KYSA-6 (myasthenia gravis) toplines. The **rolling BLA is to complete in Q4 2026**. Lupus nephritis Phase 1 data are expected in 2026 — [Kyverna PR](https://ir.kyvernatx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kyverna-therapeutics-announces-positive-topline-data); [Fierce Biotech](https://www.fiercebiotech.com/biotech/kyverna-gains-clear-view-first-car-t-approval-autoimmune-disease-after-truly-remarkable-sps)
- Allogeneic anti-CD19 CAR-T induced remission in refractory SLE (published case series) — [PMC](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12297247/)
- **In vivo CAR-T**: AbbVie bought **Capstan for $2.1B (Jun 30, 2025)**. Capstan's CPTX2309 (now ABBV-619) is a CD8-targeted tLNP delivering anti-CD19 CAR mRNA, in Phase 1 for SLE and RA — [AbbVie PR](https://news.abbvie.com/2025-06-30-AbbVie-to-Acquire-Capstan-Therapeutics,-Further-Strengthening-Commitment-to-Transforming-Patient-Care-in-Immunology). ## 5. Policy: FDA changes 2025-2026, drug pricing (MFN), China's rise in licensing

### Takeaway
The FDA under Makary has added speed tools: the Commissioner's National Priority Voucher (CNPV), with 1-2 month reviews (e.g., zongertinib approved 44 days after filing), and the plausible-mechanism pathway. Leadership has been volatile, including the departure of CBER chief Prasad. MFN pricing is being implemented through bilateral company deals (GLP-1 at $245 in Medicare), TrumpRx (launched Feb 5, 2026) and Medicaid MFN deals (Sep 2026). China-origin assets are now the main source of in-licensing for big pharma: $137.7B in 2025 total deal value, about $60B in Q1 2026, and average upfronts up 230% since 2022.

### Cited Findings
- **CNPV pilot**: aims to cut review time from 10-12 months to **1-2 months**. **Hernexeos (zongertinib)** was filed Jan 13, 2026 and approved **44 days** later.
