## 1. Carbon removal (DAC, CDR purchase volumes, Frontier/Microsoft)

### Takeaway
Buyers have contracted large volumes of durable carbon removal (CDR): 30.4 Mt in 2025 alone. Almost all of it is one buyer, Microsoft, at about 79% of all tonnes. Physical delivery is still tiny (Frontier expects about 50 kt delivered in 2026). The flagship DAC plant, Stratos, slipped from end-2024 to end-2026. Over 2027-2031 the risk is concentration: if Microsoft slows its buying, the market has no comparable backstop.

### Cited Findings
- Contracted durable CDR purchases reached **30.4 Mt in 2025**. Purchase volume grew at a **324% CAGR from 2021 to 2025** (CDR.fyi, snippet) — [CDR.fyi demand structure snapshot, Apr 2026](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/durable-cdr-demand-structure-snapshot-april-2026)
- **Microsoft held 78.5% of all disclosed durable CDR tonnes as of April 2026**, over 25 Mt contracted since 2020 (snippet). A second source gives about 79.5% — [CDR.fyi](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/durable-cdr-demand-structure-snapshot-april-2026); [Carbon Herald, CDR in 2025](https://carbonherald.com/cdr-in-2025-the-shifts-and-strides-that-redefined-the-sector/). One outlet headlines "Microsoft bought 93%", which probably refers to a narrower period — [decarbonfuse](https://decarbonfuse.com/posts/microsoft-bought-93-of-carbon-removals-now-what)
- **Buyers other than Microsoft and Frontier contracted 3.2 Mt in 2025** (snippet) — [CDR.fyi](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/durable-cdr-demand-structure-snapshot-april-2026)
- Microsoft's 2025 mega-deals, as reported:
  - AtmosClear, 6.7 Mt (BECCS)
  - CO2 Limited, 3.7 Mt
  - C2X, 3.6 Mt
  - Exomad Green, 1.24 Mt (biochar)
  
  Source: [Carbon Herald](https://carbonherald.com/cdr-in-2025-the-shifts-and-strides-that-redefined-the-sector/)
- Q2 2025 was the "biggest quarter ever" for durable CDR (CDR.fyi title). CDR.fyi's Q3 2025 update was titled "Tacking into the Wind", which signals a slowdown — [CDR.fyi Q2 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2025-q2-durable-cdr-market-update-biggest-quarter-ever); [CDR.fyi Q3 2025](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2025-q3-durable-cdr-market-update-tacking-into-the-wind)
- **Frontier, July 2026:**
  - New commitments of **$915M**; Anthropic and Salesforce joined as participants.
  - Total pledges now **$1.8B**.
  - The coalition will concentrate on fewer projects: ocean alkalinity enhancement (OAE), enhanced rock weathering (ERW) and DAC.
  
  Source: [Quartz](https://qz.com/anthropic-frontier-carbon-removal-coalition-915-million-061826); [Carbon Capture Magazine](https://carboncapturemagazine.com/articles/frontier-commits-additional-915-million-to-scale-carbon-removal-industry)
- **Frontier portfolio:**
  - Over **$713M** in offtake agreements, covering **1,886,898 t** contracted.
  - Of that, **$261M / 688,300 t** was signed since Dec 2024.
  - Portfolio suppliers expect to remove **more than 50,000 t in 2026**, described as "another year of doubled annual deliveries".
  
  Source: [Frontier via search summary / Carbon Capture Magazine](https://carboncapturemagazine.com/articles/frontier-commits-additional-915-million-to-scale-carbon-removal-industry)
- Frontier–Planetary deal: **$31.3M for more than 115,000 t via OAE, 2026-2030**. That is about $272/t, derived from the two figures — [Planetary](https://www.planetarytech.com/frontier-commits-31m-to-planetary-to-scale-ocean-carbon-removal/)
- **Stratos (1PointFive/Occidental, Texas):**
  - Design capacity is **up to 500,000 tCO2/yr**. The largest DAC plant then operating was about 36 kt/yr.
  - Construction began in May 2023. The original start was end-2024; **Oxy now says operations begin at the end of 2026**.
  - After phase-one commissioning, a problem was found in **"non-process components"**, which Oxy says is not the capture technology.
  - BlackRock invested **$550M** through a joint venture.
  
  Sources: [E&E News](https://www.eenews.net/articles/worlds-largest-carbon-removal-plant-to-open-at-the-end-of-2026-2/); [Carbon Herald](https://carbonherald.com/occidentals-stratos-dac-facility-delayed-by-unexpected-component-issue/); [Oxy](https://www.oxy.com/news/news-releases/occidental-and-blackrock-form-joint-venture-to-develop-stratos-the-worlds-largest-direct-air-capture-plant/). Some outlets (e.g., oilgasleads) report "initial operations Q2 2026". That conflicts with the end-2026 guidance, so treat the start date as uncertain.
- MOFs have **not yet entered the commercial DAC market** (IDTechEx):
  - MOF sorbent cost is $50–200/kg, against $5–20/kg for solid amines.
  - IDTechEx forecasts MOF demand will grow **50x between 2027 and 2035** (projection).
  
  Source: [IDTechEx](https://www.idtechex.com/en/research-article/metal-organic-frameworks-based-carbon-capture-current-status-and-future/33224); [PatSnap DAC 2026](https://www.patsnap.com/resources/blog/articles/direct-air-capture-technology-landscape-2026/)

### Inferences
- Contracted tonnes (~30 Mt in 2025) exceed annual deliveries (tens of kt) by roughly 100x or more. The 2027-2031 story will be about delivery and verification, not new contracts.
- Probability that Stratos reports sustained commercial operation (capturing CO2 at any meaningful rate) by end-2027: ~65%. Probability it runs at at least 50% of nameplate (250 kt/yr) for a full year by 2029: ~30%. Basis: repeated slips and first-of-a-kind scale-up.
- Probability that Microsoft's share of annual durable CDR contracting falls below 50% in any year by 2028: ~40%. It depends on Frontier's new $915M, on compliance demand (EU CRCF, Japan GX-ETS) and on new AI hyperscaler buyers.
- Leading indicators:
  - CDR.fyi quarterly tonnes delivered vs contracted
  - Oxy quarterly calls on Stratos
  - 45Q credit status under the OBBBA (see section 3)
  - Frontier price per tonne in new offtakes

### Gaps
- Exact 2026 H1 CDR.fyi contracted and delivered totals: cdr.fyi was blocked.
- Whether 45Q DAC credit values changed in the 2025 budget law: not verified.
- Climeworks Mammoth performance figures: not searched.

## 2. Green hydrogen, green steel/cement, PFAS destruction, heat pumps, EVs

### Takeaway
The adoption split is sharp:
- **EVs and electrification:** strong, led by China.
  - Over 20M EVs were sold in 2025, 25% of global car sales.
  - China reached about 55% EV share.
- **Heat pumps:** mixed. Europe recovered, while the US fell 13% and global sales fell 2%.
- **Molecule-based decarbonization is in retreat or delayed:**
  - Green hydrogen faces mass cancellations of large projects.
  - Stegra green steel needed a rescue financing.

### Cited Findings
**EVs (IEA Global EV Outlook 2026, published ~May 2026; measured data for 2025)**
- Global EV sales grew **20% to more than 20 million in 2025**, **one-quarter of all new cars** — [IEA GEVO 2026 exec summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary)
- China:
  - **More than 13 million EVs sold in 2025**, about **55% of new car sales**.
  - Growth slowed partly because of a temporary halt to the trade-in scheme.
  - IEA projects China at **almost 60% of sales in 2026** (projection).
  
  Source: [IEA GEVO 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/executive-summary)
- **Chinese automakers supplied about 60% of global EV sales in 2025** — [IEA GEVO 2026 Manufacturing and trade](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/manufacturing-and-trade)
- IEA projects **about 23M EV sales in 2026, about 28% of the global market** (projection) — [Down To Earth summarising IEA](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/air/electric-vehicle-sales-projected-to-reach-23-million-globally-in-2026-iea-report)

**Heat pumps (IEA Global Energy Review 2026 / Heat Pump Monitor 2026)**
- **Global heat pump sales fell about 2% in 2025**:
  - **Europe rose 11%**, its first growth year since 2022.
  - **US fell about 13%**.
  - China and Japan were roughly flat.
  
  Source: [IEA Global Energy Review 2026 – heat pumps](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-energy-review-2026/technology-heat-pumps); [IEA Heat Pump Monitor 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/heat-pump-monitor-2026/key-findings)
- EHPA: **2.9M units in 21 European countries in 2025, up 13%**. Germany H1 was up 55%, while France declined — [EHPA market data](https://ehpa.org/market-data/); [IIR](https://iifiir.org/en/news/european-heat-pump-market-shows-signs-of-recovery-in-2025-after-two-year-decline)

**Green hydrogen**
- More than **4.9 Mt/yr of low-emissions H2 capacity was withdrawn globally** in the 12 months to Q4 2025 — [EnkiAI summary](https://enkiai.com/biggest-hydrogen-project-cancellations-in-2025-and-2024/) (secondary source. The underlying origin is likely BNEF or IEA but was not verified.)

  - BP HyGreen Teesside, 80 MW (Dec 2025)
  - GH2, Canada, 1.2 GW (Jan 2026; no buyers)
  - Plug Power's $290M New York plant (Mar 2026)

  Sources: [EnkiAI](https://enkiai.com/hydrogen-top-10-projects-companies/); [Hydrogen Insight](https://www.hydrogeninsight.com/production/cancelled-post-fid-fortescue-abandons-two-green-hydrogen-projects-it-had-previously-signed-off-on/2-1-1849742); [Chemistry World](https://www.chemistryworld.com/news/clean-hydrogen-project-cancellations-point-to-narrower-future/4023051.article)
- One analysis says FIDs grew about **20% year on year in 2025**, but on smaller modular projects:
  - 20–690 MW in size
  - captive offtake signed before FID
  - sited next to cheap firm clean power
  
  Source: [Decarbonize Weekly](https://decarbonizeweekly.com/articles/dd007-green-hydrogen-reset/) (secondary). Deloitte's 2026 outlook reportedly puts **more than 75%** of projects in development at risk (per the same secondary source).

  Source: [Stegra press release](https://stegra.com/en/news-and-stories/stegra-has-agreed-in-principle-on-a-14-billion-financing-round); [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/stegra-secures-1-7-billion-financing-to-rescue-green-steel-project/)

### Inferences
- Probability the IEA reports global EV share of at least 30% for calendar 2027: ~60%. At least 40% by 2030: ~55%. China above 65% by 2028: ~60%. Basis: 25% in 2025 and a projected 28% in 2026, with growth slowing in China and US policy headwinds.
- Probability that Stegra produces commercial steel at Boden by end-2027: ~55%.
- Probability that global electrolyzer capacity actually reaching FID stays below 10% of the 2030 announced pipeline: high, ~80%. Expect "hydrogen hubs" to shrink to ammonia, refining and green-iron niches.
- Leading indicators:

  - BNEF electrolyzer shipment data
  - EU Hydrogen Bank auction clearing prices
  - Stegra commissioning news
  - Chinese domestic EV share after trade-in subsidies end

### Gaps
- Green cement: no data collected, e.g., Brevik CCS plant (Heidelberg) start-up in 2025 or Sublime Systems status.
- PFAS destruction commercial deployments (e.g., supercritical water oxidation, electrochemical): not researched beyond the WEF listing.
- BNEF primary hydrogen FID totals for 2025: not retrieved.

## 3. US IRA rollback (One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed 4 July 2025) and effects on clean-tech investment

### Takeaway
US clean investment has not collapsed in aggregate:
- **Q2 2026 was $75B, up 4% year on year.** Developers rushed deployment ahead of the **4 July 2026 construction-start deadlines**.
- **Cancellations hit a record**: $17B of clean electricity and industrial-decarbonization investment in Q2 2026.
- A slowdown in manufacturing and deployment is likely from 2027 onward, once the safe-harbor pipeline is used up.

### Cited Findings
- Rhodium Q2 2026 (snippet):
  - US clean energy and transport investment was **$75B in Q2 2026**, **up 22% from Q1 2026** and **up 4% from Q2 2025**.
  - About **$17B of announced clean electricity and industrial decarbonization investment was cancelled**, the highest quarter on record for that segment.
  - By technology: hydrogen $7B, storage $6B, solar $3B.
  
  Source: [Rhodium CIM Q2 2026](https://rhg.com/research/clean-investment-monitor-us-q2-2026-update/); [PDF](https://rhg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Clean-Investment-Monitor_US-Q2-2026.pdf)
- Q1 2026 saw a surge of utility-scale solar and storage announcements ahead of the **4 July 2026 OBBBA construction-eligibility deadline** — [E2 Q1 2026](https://e2.org/reports/clean-economy-works-q1-2026/)
- E2, July 2026: abandoned clean energy projects since the start of 2025 will cost **about 470,000 jobs** and hundreds of billions of dollars in GDP (advocacy group estimate) — [E2](https://e2.org/releases/cancellations-impact-report-2026/)
- CATF analysis of US Q1 2026 clean investment — [CATF](https://www.catf.us/2026/07/us-clean-energy-investments-2026-quarter-1-analysis/); Q1 manufacturing investment lagged — [Clean Investment Monitor](https://www.cleaninvestmentmonitor.org/reports/us-clean-manufacturing-investment-q1-2026)
- OBBBA provisions explainer — [SEIA](https://seia.org/research-resources/clean-energy-provisions-big-beautiful-bill/); [Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/one-big-beautiful-bill-new-law-disrupts-clean-energy-investment)

### Inferences
- Probability that US quarterly clean investment in the Rhodium CIM falls below $60B in at least one quarter of 2027: ~55%. Once safe-harbored projects are done, wind and solar deployment likely drops. Storage and nuclear/geothermal, whose credits are more protected, partly offset this.
- Leading indicators:
  - Rhodium quarterly cancellation totals
  - Treasury guidance on "beginning of construction" and FEOC rules
  - EIA planned capacity additions for 2027-28

### Gaps
- Exact trailing-4-quarter totals and the manufacturing share: rhg.com was blocked.

## 4. Advanced materials: superconductors, AI discovery, MOFs, rare earths

### Takeaway
- **Superconductors:** no room-temperature, ambient-pressure superconductor has been validated as of 2026.
- **AI-generated materials:** claims such as GNoME and A-Lab face credible critiques. Nature corrected the A-Lab paper in January 2026.
- **MOFs:** won the 2025 Chemistry Nobel but are not yet commercial in DAC.
- **Rare earths:** China's October 2025 expanded export controls are suspended only until **10 Nov 2026**, which makes that date a key near-term event.

### Cited Findings
- **LK-99** was explained by Cu2S impurities. Ferromagnetism and a superionic transition mimicked superconductivity — [Phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2023-11-myth-room-temperature-superconductivity-lk-.html); [Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/a/room-temperature-superconductor-lk-99-fails-replication-tests/)
- As of April 2026, **no material meets all five of a proposed set of validation criteria**:
  - zero resistance above 273 K
  - SQUID Meissner effect
  - specific-heat anomaly
  - three independent replications within six months
  - 24-hour ambient stability
  
  The field is split between high-Tc hydrides, which need more than 100 GPa, and unvalidated ambient-pressure claims. [PatSnap, 2026](https://www.patsnap.com/resources/blog/articles/room-temperature-superconductor-research-2026-landscape/). This is an industry blog, and the "five criteria" framing may be its own.
- **GNoME (DeepMind, 2023)** claimed about 380k stable crystals (DeepMind blog says 2.2M new, 380k most stable) — [DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/millions-of-new-materials-discovered-with-deep-learning/)
  - Critiques: a Chem. Mater. perspective found few compounds that were "credible, new and useful" — [ACS Chem. Mater.](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.chemmater.4c00643)
  - **In January 2026 Nature corrected the Berkeley A-Lab paper**, which had claimed 41 novel compounds in 17 days. The critique, led by Robert Palgrave (UCL), found most compounds were already in the ICSD — [Cypris summary](https://www.cypris.ai/insights/ai-accelerated-materials-discovery-in-2025-how-generative-models-graph-neural-networks-and-autonomous-labs-are-transforming-r-d) (secondary; verify against Nature)
  - One secondary source says fewer than 5% of GNoME proposals have been validated independently (unverified claim) — [NWI](https://nextwavesinsight.com/ai-materials-discovery-gnome-mattergen-2026/)
  - Related data-quality problem: duplicate structures in crystallography databases — [C&EN, Dec 2025](https://cen.acs.org/research-integrity/Duplicate-structures-haunt-crystallography-databases/103/web/2025/12)
  - A June 2026 preprint on pre-registered validation of ML-predicted sodium-cathode voltages is titled "Computational references are not experiments" — [arXiv 2606.23725](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.23725)
- **2025 Nobel Prize in Chemistry: Richard Robson, Susumu Kitagawa, Omar Yaghi, for MOFs.** Cited applications are water harvesting from air, pollutant removal and CO2 capture — [NobelPrize.org](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/chemistry/2025/press-release/); [C&EN](https://cen.acs.org/people/nobel-prize/The-2025-chemistry-Nobel-goes-to-MOFs/103/web/2025/10)
- **Rare earths:**
  - China's **9 Oct 2025** controls, announced for 8 Nov 2025, covered five more rare-earth elements and **extraterritorial rules on items made abroad with Chinese materials or technology, including a 0.1% content rule**.
  - These were **suspended for one year until 10 Nov 2026** after the Trump–Xi deal.
  - The **April 2025 controls on 7 medium and heavy REEs remain in force**.
  - Analysts expect one of three outcomes: extension, selective reinstatement, or full reimposition.
  
  Sources: [China Briefing](https://www.china-briefing.com/news/chinas-rare-earth-export-controls-impacts-on-businesses/); [Mining Technology](https://www.mining-technology.com/news/china-rare-earth-export-pause-nears-expiry-amid-persistent-supply-concentration/); [Pillsbury](https://www.pillsburylaw.com/en/news-and-insights/china-suspends-export-controls-certain-critical-minerals-related-items.html); [Carra Globe](https://carraglobe.com/china-rare-earth-export-controls-2026/)
- The WEF 2026 list includes **passive radiative cooling materials** and **direct lithium extraction**, both materials-led technologies — [WEF PDF](https://reports.weforum.org/docs/WEF_Top_10_Emerging_Technologies_Report_2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- Probability that an ambient-pressure room-temperature superconductor is independently replicated by 2031: ≤3%.
- Probability that China's October 2025 rare-earth rules are extended or remain suspended past 10 Nov 2026: ~60%. Probability of partial reinstatement: ~30%. Probability of full reimposition: ~10%. The trade truce has held so far, and the controls serve as leverage.
- Leading indicators:
  - MOFCOM announcements before 10 Nov 2026
  - Chinese magnet export tonnages from monthly customs data
  - Output ramps at non-Chinese separation plants (Lynas, MP Materials)
- AI materials discovery: expect the field to move toward pre-registered, experimentally validated benchmarks. Probability that an AI-discovered material (inverse-designed, not screened from known compounds) is in a commercial product by 2031: ~35%.

### Gaps
- 2D materials and metamaterials: 2025-26 commercialization milestones (e.g., graphene semiconductors, metasurface optics in phones) not researched.
- Periodic Labs, Lila Sciences and other AI-for-science lab startup funding: not retrieved.
- Rare-earth price data: not retrieved.

## 5. Cross-cutting: R&D budgets, US science cuts, Nature Index, VC allocation, talent

### Takeaway
- **Capital:** it is extremely concentrated in AI. **About 86% of US VC and over 70% of global VC went to AI in H1 2026**, and OpenAI plus Anthropic took about 43% of global startup funding.
- **US public science:** the administration again proposed deep cuts for FY2027: NSF −55%, NIH −12–13%, DOE Office of Science −15%. Congress is again resisting.
- **Research output:** China widened its Nature Index lead, with 22.4% output growth in 2025 and 53% of chemistry Share.
- **Talent:** US-based researchers are applying abroad in larger numbers.

### Cited Findings
- **PitchBook-NVCA, Q2 2026 (published about July 2026):**
  - US VC was **$412.7B in H1 2026**, with **$355.9B (86%) going to AI**.
  - **Megadeals of $100M or more took 87.5%** (Fortune).
  
  Source: [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/09/pitchbook-us-venture-funding-hits-412-7b-first-half-ai-deals-dominate/); [Fortune, 12 Aug 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/venture-capital-funding-ai-pitchbook-us-vc-valuations-series-d/); [PitchBook Q2 2026 Venture Monitor](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q2-2026-pitchbook-nvca-venture-monitor)
- **More than 70% of global VC went to AI in H1 2026**. OpenAI ($122B) and Anthropic ($95.6B) together took about 43% of global startup funding (secondary aggregator) — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/us-venture-hits-4127b-in-h1-2026-ai-takes-86); [PitchBook Global VC First Look Q2 2026](https://pitchbook.com/news/reports/q2-2026-global-vc-first-look)

### Inferences
- All three 2026 trend lists moved from pure AI toward physical and industrial deployment: grids, lithium, PFAS, nuclear, sodium-ion, factories. That matches the "AI meets atoms" theme, while VC dollars remain concentrated in AI model labs. Non-AI deep tech (climate, materials) is capital-starved in relative terms. That is a structural headwind for 2027-2031 climate and materials startups unless an AI-infrastructure link exists, such as data-center power, cooling, or critical minerals.
- Probability that FY2027 enacted NSF appropriations come in above $7B (i.e., Congress again rejects the ~55% cut): ~75%. Probability that China's Nature Index Share exceeds the US's in every one of the 7 subject areas, including health sciences, by the 2030 edition: ~35%.
- Probability that AI's share of global VC stays above 50% in every year 2027-2028: ~70%. A correction in AI valuations is the main downside risk.
- Leading indicators:
  - PitchBook quarterly AI share
  - FY2027 appropriations outcome (a continuing resolution is likely; the fiscal year starts 1 Oct 2026)
  - NIH indirect-cost cap litigation
  - ERC and Canadian applicant counts
  - H-1B and visa policy
  - Nature Index 2027 (June 2027)
