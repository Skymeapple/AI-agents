# Energy Technology: State as of September 2026 and Outlook for 2027-2031

Research date: 2026-09-24. Tags: [MEASURED] = measured or delivered result; [CLAIM] = company roadmap, target or forecast. Probabilities are the researcher's own judgments based on the cited evidence. They are not taken from the sources.

## Batteries: pack prices, sodium-ion, solid-state, LFP, grid storage

### Takeaway
Battery costs keep falling while metal prices rise. In December 2025 BNEF reported an average pack price of $108/kWh, LFP at $81/kWh and stationary storage at $70/kWh. Grid storage is growing about 40-50% a year: 112 GW/307 GWh in 2025 and a forecast 158 GW/459 GWh in 2026. Sodium-ion reached GWh-scale commercial production in 2026 (CATL Naxtra). Semi-solid-state batteries are shipping in cheap Chinese cars. True all-solid-state batteries are still at the pilot-line stage, with mass production targeted for 2027-2028.

### Cited Findings
**Pack prices (BNEF survey, published 2025-12-09)**
- [MEASURED] The volume-weighted average Li-ion pack price fell 8% from 2024 to a record low of $108/kWh in 2025 — [BNEF](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/); [pv magazine 2025-12-09](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/12/09/global-lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-kwh-says-bnef/)
- [MEASURED] Stationary storage packs cost $70/kWh in 2025, 45% less than in 2024, making it the cheapest segment. BEV packs cost $99/kWh, the cheapest transport segment — [ESS News 2025-12-09](https://www.ess-news.com/2025/12/09/bnef-lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-kwh-stationary-storage-becomes-lowest-price-segment/)
- [MEASURED] LFP packs averaged $81/kWh across all segments, against $128/kWh for NMC. China had the lowest average at $84/kWh. North America was 44% higher and Europe 56% higher — [pv magazine](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2025/12/09/global-lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-kwh-says-bnef/)
- Stated drivers: cell overcapacity, strong competition and the shift to LFP, which together outweighed higher lithium and cobalt prices — [BNEF](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/)

**Grid storage deployment (BNEF)**
- [MEASURED] 112 GW/307 GWh of storage was added globally in 2025, up 48% on 2024. China added 61.1 GW/173.1 GWh and the US added 18 GW/54.6 GWh, together about 70% of the total. Sub-Saharan Africa added 4.3 GW/8.8 GWh, four times its 2024 figure — [Energy-Storage.News](https://www.energy-storage.news/bloombergnef-forecasts-158gw-of-global-energy-storage-deployments-in-2026/); [pv magazine 2026-05-11](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/05/11/bloombergnef-confirms-energy-storage-has-reached-the-100-gw-era/)
- [CLAIM/forecast] BNEF forecasts 158 GW/459 GWh for 2026 (up 41%), annual additions of 308 GW by 2036, and cumulative capacity of 2.9 TW/10.5 TWh by end-2036 — [Energy-Storage.News](https://www.energy-storage.news/bloombergnef-forecasts-158gw-of-global-energy-storage-deployments-in-2026/)

### Inferences
- **2026 BNEF survey (due about December 2026): will the average pack price be at or below $100/kWh?** Estimated probability: about 55%. The 8% decline in 2025 would put the 2026 figure near $99-100, but higher lithium prices could slow the decline. Probability the average is at or below $90/kWh by the 2028 survey: about 60%. Probability LFP stationary packs are at or below $60/kWh by 2027: about 55%.
- **Annual global storage additions of at least 400 GWh in 2026:** about 80%. Probability that annual additions reach at least 1 TWh in some year by 2030: about 50%. Reaching it requires roughly 35% compound annual growth.
- **Sodium-ion cumulative shipments of at least 100 GWh by 2030:** about 45%. The 60 GWh HyperStrong deal is the main support. The main risk is that sodium-ion's cost edge depends on lithium prices.
- **An all-solid-state (not semi-solid) battery in a series-production car on sale to consumers by end-2027:** about 35%. By end-2028: about 65%, with Chinese automakers (SAIC, GAC) and Samsung SDI/BMW the likeliest first. Probability of a Toyota solid-state car on sale by end-2028: about 35%.
- **QuantumScape cells in a customer vehicle (beyond demonstration fleets) by end-2028:** about 30%.
- **Leading indicators:** lithium carbonate spot price; BNEF December surveys; CATL and BYD sodium-ion GWh shipment disclosures; Samsung SDI Ulsan line start-up; QuantumScape "B-sample" and "C-sample" shipments in its 10-Q filings; China's national solid-state battery standard (terminology separating "semi-solid" from true solid-state).

## Fusion

### Takeaway
Measured physics progress is real but still at the experimental stage. In April 2025 NIF set records of 8.6 MJ yield and a target gain of 4.13. Private funding reached a record $4.48 bn in the 12 months to July 2026, bringing the total to $14.24 bn. Commercial dates are still company claims: Helion promises Microsoft at least 50 MW by 2028, and CFS aims for SPARC first plasma in about 2026-2027 and net energy (Q>1) in about 2027. ITER will not start research operations until 2034 or deuterium-tritium operation until 2039.

### Cited Findings
- [MEASURED] On 2025-04-07 NIF produced 8.6 MJ from 2.08 MJ of laser energy (456 TW peak). That is a target gain of 4.13 and NIF's eighth ignition shot. The previous record was 5.2 MJ in February 2024 — [LLNL NIF](https://lasers.llnl.gov/about/keys-to-success/nif-sets-power-energy-records); [LLNL FY2025 annual report](https://annual.llnl.gov/fy-2025/national-ignition-facility-2025). "Gain" here is target gain only. Wall-plug energy input is roughly 100 times higher, so there is no net electrical gain.
- [MEASURED] The FIA 2026 report counts $4.48 bn raised in the 12 months to July 2026, a record, and a cumulative $14.24 bn since the survey began in 2021. It covers 56 companies (23 in 2021) and more than 16,000 employees — [FIA press release](https://www.fusionindustryassociation.org/fusion-industry-attracts-record-annual-funding-of-4-48bn-raising-total-to-14-24bn/); [Energy Live News 2026-07-22](https://www.energylivenews.com/2026/07/22/fusion-sector-secures-record-4-48bn-in-funding/)
- **CFS SPARC:**
  - [MEASURED] The first of 18 HTS toroidal-field magnets (TF1) has been installed, the first 48-ton half of the vacuum vessel has been delivered, and a radioactive materials license has been granted — [CFS](https://cfs.energy/news-and-media/commonwealth-fusion-systems-granted-radioactive-materials-license-for-sparc-fusion-machine/); [Prism News](https://www.prismnews.com/hobbies/nuclear-reactions/commonwealth-fusion-systems-installs-first-sparc-magnet-eyes-2026-plasma-milestone)
  - [CLAIM] Timing is inconsistent across sources. CFS has long given first plasma as a 2026 target and net energy (Q>1) as 2027, while some secondary 2026 reporting says first plasma has slipped to 2027 — [The Fusion Report](https://thefusionreport.com/commonwealth-fusion-systems-a-hot-start-for-2026/); [Wikipedia SPARC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPARC_(tokamak)). SPARC is designed for Q>10 and 50-100 MW of fusion power. The ARC power plant is planned for Chesterfield County, Virginia (early 2030s).
- **Helion:**
  - [CLAIM] Polaris (7th generation) is reportedly the first private fusion machine run on D-T fuel, with plasma above 150 million °C. These are company-reported figures with no peer-reviewed electricity demonstration yet — [Helion](https://www.helionenergy.com/newsroom/helion-achieves-new-fusion-energy-milestones)
  - [MEASURED] Construction of Orion (50 MW, Malaga, Washington) began in July 2025, under a PPA to deliver at least 50 MW to Microsoft by 2028 — [NEI Magazine](https://www.neimagazine.com/news/helion-advances-fusion-energy-program/); [CarbonCredits](https://carboncredits.com/microsoft-msft-to-get-fusion-power-as-helion-energy-kicks-off-orion-plant-construction/)
  - [MEASURED] Helion raised a $465 M Series G at a $15.5 bn post-money valuation on 2026-06-09 — [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/helion-hits-15-5b-valuation-with-465m-in-new-cash-to-commercialize-fusion-this-decade/)
  - [MEASURED] On 2026-06-16 the Washington State Department of Health issued a Radioactive Materials License and a Radioactive Air Emissions License for Orion, which Helion calls the first fusion power-plant licenses anywhere — [IndustrialSage](https://www.industrialsage.com/helion-energy-first-fusion-regulatory-licenses-2026/)
- [MEASURED/official schedule] ITER's 2024 baseline sets start of research operation (SRO) in 2034, full magnetic energy with D-D in 2036, and D-T operation in 2039. The previous plan had first plasma in 2025. The delay adds about €5 bn in cost — [IPP](https://www.ipp.mpg.de/5434926/ITER_baseline_2024); [Physics World](https://physicsworld.com/a/iter-fusion-reactor-hit-by-massive-decade-long-delay-and-e5bn-price-hike/)
- China BEST (Hefei): civil works have been under way since 2023. Secondary reporting puts completion at about 2027 with a burning-plasma/Q>1 goal later in the decade. China has also tested a 582-ton superconducting magnet for fusion — [Autonocion (secondary, low reliability)](https://www.autonocion.com/us/china-tested-superconducting-magnet-largest-ever-built-fusion/). I did not find a primary source (CAS/ASIPP) in this session.

### Inferences
- **SPARC first plasma by 2026-12-31:** about 20%. By end-2027: about 70%. SPARC Q>1 (scientific breakeven in a magnetic-confinement device) announced by end-2028: about 35%. By end-2030: about 60%.
- **Helion delivering any fusion electricity to the grid for Microsoft by end-2028:** about 10%. Probability Helion publicly shows net electricity (electricity out greater than electricity in) from Polaris by end-2027: about 20%.
- **A new NIF record above 8.6 MJ or target gain above 4.13 by end-2027:** about 65%, given the steady stream of record shots.
- **ITER keeps SRO in 2034 (no new slip announced by 2031):** about 50%.
- **FIA 2027 report showing annual funding above $4.48 bn:** about 55%. Mega-rounds such as Helion's and CFS's make the annual figure lumpy.
- **Leading indicators:** CFS announcing all 18 TF magnets installed and cooldown; Helion peer-reviewed data on energy recovery; DOE Milestone Program payments; China BEST assembly news; NRC Part 30 fusion rule finalization.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the exact SPARC schedule as of September 2026 because sources conflict.
- There are no primary EAST 2025-2026 records in these notes (for example, the January 2025 1,066-second H-mode run was not verified in this session).
- There is no BEST primary schedule.
- TAE, Tokamak Energy and General Fusion status were not covered.

## Advanced fission, SMRs, restarts and hyperscaler deals

### Takeaway
2026 is the year licensing turned into construction. The NRC issued its first-ever construction permit for a non-light-water commercial reactor (TerraPower Natrium, 2026-03-04). The X-energy/Dow Long Mott permit is expected in Q1 2027. Hyperscaler contracts now total many GW: Meta up to 6.6 GW, Amazon about 1.26 GW plus a 5 GW SMR pipeline, Google about 1.1 GW. The Palisades restart is in its final startup steps (fuel loaded from 2026-08-30). The Crane (TMI-1) restart is targeted for late 2027.

### Cited Findings
- [MEASURED] The NRC Commission voted on 2026-03-04 to issue the construction permit for Natrium Kemmerer Unit 1 (Wyoming). It is the first NRC construction permit for a commercial non-LWR power reactor. The design is a 345 MWe sodium fast reactor with molten-salt storage that can boost output to 500 MWe — [TerraPower](https://www.terrapower.com/NRC-Approves-Natrium-Reactor-Construction-Permit); [DOE](https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/nrc-issues-construction-permit-terrapowers-natrium-advanced-reactor); [ANS](https://www.ans.org/news/article-7818/nrc-approves-terrapower-construction-permit/)

In August 2025 Kairos and TVA signed a PPA under which Hermes 2 will supply the grid for Google data centers. Google has committed to 500 MW of Kairos KP-FHR capacity — [Wikipedia Kairos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kairos_Power); [smrintel](https://smrintel.com/nuclear-data-center-deals/)

## Enhanced geothermal and perovskite-silicon tandem solar

### Takeaway
Enhanced geothermal crossed from pilot to utility scale. Fervo's Cape Station reached first power on 2026-09-24, with its first 33 MW unit due for commercial operation on 2026-10-01 and about 500 MW planned by 2028. Perovskite-silicon tandem cells set a certified record of 35.5% (LONGi, July 2026). Commercial modules still trail cells: Oxford PV's certified residential module is 26.9%, and LONGi has no public mass-production date.

### Cited Findings
- [MEASURED] Fervo Cape Station (Beaver County, Utah) reached "First Power" on 2026-09-24, the first utility-scale enhanced geothermal system (EGS) to sync to the grid anywhere — [GlobeNewswire 2026-09-24](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/24/3368115/0/en/fervo-energy-achieves-first-power-at-cape-station-a-landmark-moment-for-the-future-of-enhanced-geothermal-systems.html)
- [CLAIM/schedule] Phase I is about 100 MW (3 × 33 MW units). Unit 1 is due for commercial operation by 2026-10-01 and units 2-3 by 2027-01-01. A further 400 MW phase is under construction with commercial operation in 2028. About 900 MW of offtake is contracted. Fervo IPO'd in May 2026 (ticker FRVO) — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/24/3368115/0/en/fervo-energy-achieves-first-power-at-cape-station-a-landmark-moment-for-the-future-of-enhanced-geothermal-systems.html); [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/FRVO/fervo-energy-achieves-first-power-at-cape-station-a-landmark-moment-mrvv88wvcy0t.html). - [MEASURED] LONGi set a certified 35.5% record for a two-terminal perovskite-silicon tandem cell (certified by ESTI/JRC and announced 2026-07-14). Its record history runs 33.9% (November 2023), 34.6% (June 2024), 34.85% (April 2025, NREL-certified), 35.2%, then 35.5% — [pv magazine 2026-07-15](https://www.pv-magazine.com/2026/07/15/longi-sets-new-world-record-with-35-5-efficient-perovskite-silicon-tandem-cell/); [LONGi](https://www.longi.com/en/news/crystalline-silicon-perovskite-tandem-solar-cell-new-world-efficiency-2026/)

LONGi had no public mass-production timeline for tandems as of March 2026 — [Earth Energy Log (secondary)](https://earthenergylog.com/articles/perovskite-tandem-solar-2026); [Wikipedia Oxford PV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Photovoltaics)

### Inferences
- **Fervo Cape Unit 1 declared commercial by 2026-12-31:** about 85%. Probability Phase I's full 100 MW is commercial by mid-2027: about 70%. Probability the 400 MW Phase II is at least 50% online by end-2028: about 45%.
- **Certified perovskite-silicon tandem cell record of at least 36% by end-2027:** about 55%. At least 37% by end-2030: about 45%. The recent pace is about 0.3-0.6 percentage points a year.
- **GW-scale tandem module shipments (at least 1 GW/yr from any maker) by 2029:** about 35%. Long-term durability and bankability are the main barriers.
- **Leading indicators:** Fervo quarterly reports on well flow rates and capacity factor; the next EGS projects (for example Utah FORGE and Sage Geosystems); NREL chart updates; IEC 61215 certification and field-degradation data for tandem modules; announcements of GW-scale tandem lines from LONGi, Jinko or Hanwha Qcells.

## Datacenter and AI electricity demand

### Takeaway
The IEA (April 2025) projects data-centre electricity to more than double, from about 415 TWh (2024) to about 945 TWh by 2030 and about 1,200 TWh by 2035, with the US accounting for nearly half of US demand growth. LBNL projects US data centres at 325-580 TWh by 2028, or 6.7-12% of US electricity, up from 176 TWh (4.4%) in 2023. This demand is the main commercial driver behind nuclear restarts, SMR PPAs and fusion PPAs.

### Cited Findings
